Refund Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Short version: if ImatPath is not right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase and we will refund you.

Self-Study

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase. Send us a short email from the address of your account. We do not need a long explanation. After a refund your access is closed.

Application Guide

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, as long as we have not started the one-to-one work on your application. Once we have started, for example by reviewing your documents or choosing universities with you, the service has been delivered and is no longer refundable, except where the law gives you that right.

How to ask

Email imatpathpro@gmail.com with the email you used at checkout and your order reference from the receipt. We answer within a few business days. Refunds go back to your original payment method through our payment provider, and can take several days to appear on your statement.

Technical problems

If something does not work, write to us first. We will fix it, and if we cannot, we will refund you, including after the 14 days when the problem is on our side.

Your legal rights

This policy does not reduce any consumer right you have under the law of your country.
