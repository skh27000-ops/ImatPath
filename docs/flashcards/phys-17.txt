phys17-010	A line of resistance 2.0 <i>Ω</i> carries 50 MW of power. What is the power lost in the line if the power is transmitted at 400 kV instead of 25 kV?<br><br><br>A) 500 kW instead of 8.0 MW<br>B) 31 kW instead of 125 kW<br>C) 8.0 MW instead of 31 kW<br>D) 2.0 MW instead of 8.0 MW<br>E) 31 kW instead of 8.0 MW	<b>E) 31 kW instead of 8.0 MW</b><br><br>At 25 kV: <i>I = 50 × 10<sup>6</sup>/25 × 10<sup>3</sup> = 2000</i> A, loss <i>= I<sup>2</sup>R = 8.0</i> MW. At 400 kV: <i>I = 125</i> A, loss <i>= 125<sup>2</sup> × 2.0 = 31</i> kW. The current falls 16 times and the loss 256 times.	ImatPath Physics practice
