chem01-001	<b>Element</b>: what is it?	An <b>element</b> is a pure substance made of <b>only one kind of atom</b>. It cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical reactions.	ImatPath Chemistry definition
chem01-002	<b>Compound</b>: what is it?	A <b>compound</b> is a pure substance made of <b>two or more elements chemically bonded</b> together in a <b>fixed ratio</b>. A compound has its own properties, usually very different from the elements it contains.	ImatPath Chemistry definition
chem01-003	<b>Mixture</b>: what is it?	A <b>mixture</b> contains <b>two or more substances that are not chemically bonded</b>. Each substance keeps its own properties, and the proportions can vary.	ImatPath Chemistry definition
chem01-051	<b>particle</b>: what is it?	In this course the word <b>particle</b> means any of these three.	ImatPath Chemistry term
chem01-052	<b>phase</b>: what is it?	A <b>phase</b> is a part of a sample that has the <b>same composition and properties throughout</b>, and is separated from other parts by a visible boundary.	ImatPath Chemistry term
chem01-053	<b>solution</b>: what is it?	A homogeneous mixture is also called a <b>solution</b>.	ImatPath Chemistry term
chem01-054	<b>Diffusion</b>: what is it?	<b>Diffusion</b> also happens in liquids, only much more slowly.	ImatPath Chemistry term

chem01-014	Key points: <b>Matter</b>	• <b>Element</b>: one kind of atom. <b>Compound</b>: different atoms bonded in a fixed ratio. <b>Mixture</b>: substances not bonded, any proportion.<br>• Compounds are split only by chemical reactions. Mixtures by physical methods.<br>• Symbols: <b>Co</b> is a metal, <b>CO</b> is a gas.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem01-015	Key points: <b>Systems</b>	• <b>Phase</b>: uniform part with a visible boundary.<br>• <b>Homogeneous</b> = one phase (solutions, air). <b>Heterogeneous</b> = two or more phases (oil and water, ice and water).<br>• Count <b>phases</b>, not substances.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem01-016	Key points: <b>States and energy</b>	• Solid: fixed shape and volume. Liquid: fixed volume only. Gas: neither.<br>• Temperature measures <b>average kinetic energy</b>. Same temperature: same energy, lighter particles move faster.<br>• <i>T(K) = θ(<sup>°</sup>C) + 273</i>	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem01-017	Key points: <b>Changes of state</b>	• Melting, freezing, evaporation or boiling, condensation, <b>sublimation</b> (solid to gas), <b>deposition</b> (gas to solid).<br>• Boiling happens throughout the liquid at the boiling point. Evaporation only at the surface.<br>• Boiling point <b>falls</b> when pressure falls.<br>• On a heating curve, the <b>flat parts</b> are the changes of state.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem01-006	How much heat is needed to change 50 g of ice at <i>-10 <sup>°</sup></i>C into water at <i>20 <sup>°</sup></i>C? Use <i>c<sub>ice</sub> = 2.09</i> J/(g K), <i>c<sub>water</sub> = 4.18</i> J/(g K) and latent heat of fusion 334 J/g.<br><br><br>A) 5.22 kJ<br>B) 21.9 kJ<br>C) 20.9 kJ<br>D) 16.7 kJ<br>E) 21900 kJ	<b>B) 21.9 kJ</b><br><br>Three steps. Warm the ice to 0 °C: <i>50 × 2.09 × 10 = 1045</i> J. Melt it: <i>50 × 334 = 16 700</i> J. Warm the water to 20 °C: <i>50 × 4.18 × 20 = 4180</i> J. Total <i>= 21 925</i> J <i>= 21.9</i> kJ.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
