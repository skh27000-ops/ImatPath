bio17-010	Key points: <b>Chromosomal theory</b>	• Genes are on chromosomes. Morgan's white-eyed flies: eye colour is <b>X-linked</b>.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio17-011	Key points: <b>X-linked</b>	• Males XY (hemizygous), females XX. Affected male's daughters are carriers. <b>No father-to-son</b> transmission.<br>• Carrier mother × normal father: <b>50% sons affected</b>, 50% daughters carriers.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio17-012	Key points: <b>Gene interactions</b>	• <b>Epistasis</b> (one gene masks another), <b>pleiotropy</b> (one gene, many traits), <b>polygenic</b> (many genes, one trait).<br>• Environment affects phenotype. <b>Mitochondrial inheritance</b> is maternal.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio17-013	Key points: <b>Chi-squared</b>	• χ² = Σ (O − E)² / E. Degrees of freedom = classes − 1. Below critical value (3.84 for 1 d.f. at 5%) = fits.	ImatPath Biology summary

bio17-004	Skin colour is controlled by three genes, each with two alleles that add equally (A, B, C add pigment). Two AaBbCc people have a child. What fraction of their children have exactly three pigment alleles?<br><br><br>A) 5/16<br>B) 1/64<br>C) 20/16<br>D) 3/8<br>E) 1/8	<b>A) 5/16</b><br><br>There are 6 alleles in total, each with probability 1/2 of being a pigment allele. <i>P(3 out of 6) = 20/2<sup>6</sup> = 20/64 = 5/16</i>, where 20 is the number of ways to choose 3 of the 6 alleles. Polygenic traits give a bell-shaped distribution.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio17-006	A child inherits a deletion of the chromosome 15q11–q13 region from the father and has Prader-Willi syndrome. If the same deletion came from the mother, the child would have Angelman syndrome. What does this show?<br><br><br>A) Non-disjunction<br>B) Mitochondrial inheritance<br>C) Epistasis<br>D) Incomplete dominance<br>E) Genomic imprinting: the effect of a gene depends on which parent it came from	<b>E) Genomic imprinting: the effect of a gene depends on which parent it came from</b><br><br>In genomic imprinting some genes are silenced (by methylation) depending on the parent of origin. The deletion removes the only active copy, and which genes are active differs between the paternal and maternal chromosome.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio17-008	Himalayan rabbits have dark fur on the ears, nose and feet. When a patch of white back fur is shaved and kept cold, the new fur is dark. What does this show?<br><br><br>A) The fur colour gene mutated<br>B) Fur colour is determined only by temperature<br>C) The rabbit is a different species<br>D) The phenotype depends on the interaction of genotype and environment (temperature)<br>E) The environment has no effect on phenotype	<b>D) The phenotype depends on the interaction of genotype and environment (temperature)</b><br><br>The enzyme that makes the dark pigment is active only at low temperatures. The same genotype gives a different phenotype in a different environment: dark in cold areas, white in warm ones.	ImatPath Biology practice
