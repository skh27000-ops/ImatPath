chem12-020	<small>Concentration</small><br><b>Parts per million, ppm</b><br><i>Definition · Unit?</i>	<b>Definition:</b> mg of solute per kg (or per litre) of solution<br><b>Unit:</b> ppm	ImatPath Chemistry recall
chem12-021	<small>Solutions in the body</small><br><b>Isotonic (0.9% NaCl)</b><br><i>Compared with a red blood cell · Effect on the cell?</i>	<b>Compared with a red blood cell:</b> Same osmolarity<br><b>Effect on the cell:</b> No change	ImatPath Chemistry recall
chem12-022	<small>Solutions in the body</small><br><b>Hypotonic (pure water)</b><br><i>Compared with a red blood cell · Effect on the cell?</i>	<b>Compared with a red blood cell:</b> Lower<br><b>Effect on the cell:</b> Water enters: the cell swells and may burst	ImatPath Chemistry recall
chem12-023	<small>Solutions in the body</small><br><b>Hypertonic (strong salt)</b><br><i>Compared with a red blood cell · Effect on the cell?</i>	<b>Compared with a red blood cell:</b> Higher<br><b>Effect on the cell:</b> Water leaves: the cell shrinks	ImatPath Chemistry recall
chem12-024	<small>Water as a solvent</small><br><b>Ionic solids</b>	water molecules surround each ion, with the <b>oxygen end</b> towards positive ions and the <b>hydrogen ends</b> towards negative ions. This <b>ion-dipole attraction</b> pulls the ions out of the lattice. The ions end up separated and surrounded by water: they are <b>hydrated</b>.	ImatPath Chemistry key idea
chem12-025	<small>Water as a solvent</small><br><b>Polar molecules</b>	they form <b>hydrogen bonds</b> with water and mix easily.	ImatPath Chemistry key idea
chem12-026	<small>Water as a solvent</small><br><b>Non-polar substances</b>	water cannot attract them strongly enough to separate them. They do not dissolve.	ImatPath Chemistry key idea
chem12-001	Formula to know: <b>Concentration</b>	<i>c = (n)/(V) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; n = c × V</i>	ImatPath Chemistry formula
chem12-002	Formula to know: <b>Dilution</b>	<i>c<sub>1</sub> V<sub>1</sub> = c<sub>2</sub> V<sub>2</sub></i>	ImatPath Chemistry formula
chem12-011	Key points: <b>Water as solvent</b>	• Polar water surrounds ions (ion-dipole) and forms hydrogen bonds with polar solutes.<br>• <b>Like dissolves like.</b> Electrolytes make ions and conduct.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem12-012	Key points: <b>Solubility</b>	• Saturated = maximum dissolved at that temperature.<br>• Solids: more soluble when <b>hot</b>. Gases: more soluble when <b>cold</b> and at <b>high pressure</b>.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem12-013	Key points: <b>Concentration</b>	• <i>c = n/V</i> (mol/L), <i>n = cV</i>. mL → L.<br>• Mass percent: g solute per 100 g solution. ppm: mg per kg (or per L).<br>• Ion concentration: 0.10 mol/L CaCl<sub>2</sub> gives 0.20 mol/L Cl<sup>-</sup>.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem12-014	Key points: <b>Dilution</b>	• <i>c<sub>1</sub> V<sub>1</sub> = c<sub>2</sub> V<sub>2</sub></i> (the amount of solute is unchanged).	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem12-015	Key points: <b>Body fluids</b>	• Osmosis: water moves to the side with more dissolved particles.<br>• Isotonic (0.9% NaCl): no change. Hypotonic: cell swells. Hypertonic: cell shrinks.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem12-003	50.0 cm<sup>3</sup> of 12.0 mol/L HCl are diluted with water to a final volume of 2.00 L. What is the new concentration?<br><br><br>A) 0.600 mol/L<br>B) 0.300 mol/L<br>C) 0.150 mol/L<br>D) 3.00 mol/L<br>E) 0.0300 mol/L	<b>B) 0.300 mol/L</b><br><br><i>n = 0.0500 × 12.0 = 0.600</i> mol (unchanged by dilution). <i>c = 0.600/2.00 = 0.300</i> mol/L.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem12-005	Concentrated hydrochloric acid is 37% HCl by mass and has a density of 1.19 g/cm<sup>3</sup>. What is its concentration? (<i>M = 36.5</i> g/mol)<br><br><br>A) 12.1 mol/L<br>B) 10.1 mol/L<br>C) 36.5 mol/L<br>D) 1.21 mol/L<br>E) 14.3 mol/L	<b>A) 12.1 mol/L</b><br><br>In 1.00 L: mass of solution <i>= 1190</i> g. Mass of HCl <i>= 0.37 × 1190 = 440</i> g. <i>n = 440/36.5 = 12.1</i> mol, so <i>c = 12.1</i> mol/L.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem12-007	The solubility of NaCl is 36 g per 100 g of water at 20 °C. What mass of NaCl is in 500 g of saturated solution at 20 °C?<br><br><br>A) 180 g<br>B) 36 g<br>C) 100 g<br>D) 71 g<br>E) 132 g	<b>E) 132 g</b><br><br>136 g of saturated solution contain 36 g of NaCl (100 g water + 36 g salt). For 500 g: <i>500 × 36/136 = 132</i> g.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem12-009	250 cm<sup>3</sup> of 0.10 mol/L Al<sub>2</sub>(SO<sub>4</sub>)<sub>3</sub> are prepared. What is the concentration of sulfate ions?<br><br><br>A) 0.10 mol/L<br>B) 0.20 mol/L<br>C) 0.60 mol/L<br>D) 0.30 mol/L<br>E) 0.075 mol/L	<b>D) 0.30 mol/L</b><br><br>Each formula unit gives 3 sulfate ions: <i>c(SO<sub>4</sub><sup>2-</sup>) = 3 × 0.10 = 0.30</i> mol/L. The volume does not change the concentration. (The Al<sup>3+</sup> concentration is 0.20 mol/L.)	ImatPath Chemistry practice
