bio22-013	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Autosomal recessive</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> Both sexes equally. Affected people usually have <b>unaffected carrier parents</b>. Can <b>skip generations</b>. Higher risk in <b>consanguineous</b> (related) parents<br><b>Example:</b> Cystic fibrosis, sickle cell disease	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-014	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Autosomal dominant</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> Both sexes. <b>Every affected person has an affected parent</b> (unless new mutation). <b>No skipped generations</b>. Each child of an affected heterozygote has <b>50%</b> risk<br><b>Example:</b> Huntington disease, Marfan syndrome	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-015	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>X-linked recessive</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> <b>Mostly males</b> affected. Passed through <b>carrier mothers</b>. <b>No father-to-son</b> transmission<br><b>Example:</b> Haemophilia A, red-green colour blindness	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-016	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>X-linked dominant</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> Affected fathers pass it to <b>all daughters</b> and <b>no sons</b>. Rare<br><b>Example:</b> Vitamin D-resistant rickets	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-017	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Y-linked</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> Only males, <b>father to all sons</b><br><b>Example:</b> Few genes (SRY)	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-018	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Mitochondrial</b><br><i>Typical features · Example?</i>	<b>Typical features:</b> Passed by the <b>mother to all children</b><br><b>Example:</b> Some myopathies	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-019	<small>Testing and counselling</small><br><b>Carrier testing</b><br><i>Purpose?</i>	Finds healthy carriers of recessive alleles before having children	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-020	<small>Testing and counselling</small><br><b>Prenatal testing</b><br><i>Purpose?</i>	<b>Amniocentesis</b> (fluid sample at 15–18 weeks) or <b>chorionic villus sampling (CVS)</b> (placenta, 10–13 weeks) can give a karyotype or DNA test of the fetus. Non-invasive tests use fetal DNA in the mother's blood	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-021	<small>Testing and counselling</small><br><b>Newborn screening</b><br><i>Purpose?</i>	Heel-prick test for <b>phenylketonuria (PKU)</b>, cystic fibrosis and others, so treatment begins at once	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-022	<small>Testing and counselling</small><br><b>Presymptomatic testing</b><br><i>Purpose?</i>	For adult-onset diseases such as Huntington disease	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-023	<small>Testing and counselling</small><br><b>Preimplantation genetic diagnosis (PGD)</b><br><i>Purpose?</i>	Tests embryos from IVF before transfer	ImatPath Biology recall
bio22-024	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Monogenic (Mendelian)</b>	a trait or disease is caused by <b>one gene</b>, and follows clear patterns in families (cystic fibrosis, Huntington disease).	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio22-025	<small>Patterns of inheritance</small><br><b>Multifactorial</b>	<b>many genes</b> and the <b>environment</b> both contribute (height, type 2 diabetes, coronary heart disease, most cancers). There is no simple ratio, but the risk is higher in relatives.	ImatPath Biology key idea

bio22-027	<small>Answers & explanations</small><br><b>Hardy–Weinberg</b>	p + q = 1, p² + 2pq + q² = 1. Start from <b>q²</b>, take the square root.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio22-001	Formula to know: <b>Hardy–Weinberg</b>	p + q = 1  and  p² + 2pq + q² = 1	ImatPath Biology formula
bio22-010	Key points: <b>Patterns</b>	• <b>Autosomal recessive</b>: unaffected carrier parents, can skip generations. <b>Dominant</b>: every affected person has an affected parent. <b>X-linked recessive</b>: mostly males, no father-to-son. <b>Mitochondrial</b>: from the mother.<br>• <b>Multifactorial</b>: many genes + environment.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio22-011	Key points: <b>Risk</b>	• Carrier × carrier: 1/4 affected, 1/2 carrier, <b>2/3 of unaffected are carriers</b>.<br>• <b>Hardy–Weinberg</b>: p + q = 1, p² + 2pq + q² = 1. Start from <b>q²</b>, take the square root.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio22-012	Key points: <b>Testing</b>	• Carrier, prenatal (CVS 10–13 weeks, amniocentesis 15–18), newborn screening, PGD. Twin studies separate genes from environment.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio22-002	Cystic fibrosis affects 1 in 2500 newborns of a population in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. What is the approximate carrier frequency?<br><br><br>A) About 1 in 50 (2%)<br>B) About 1 in 26 (3.9%)<br>C) About 1 in 2500<br>D) About 1 in 10 (10%)<br>E) About 1 in 100 (1%)	<b>B) About 1 in 26 (3.9%)</b><br><br><i>q<sup>2</sup> = 1/2500</i>, so <i>q = 0.02</i> and <i>p = 0.98</i>. Carriers <i>= 2pq = 2 × 0.98 × 0.02 = 0.039</i>, about 1 in 26.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio22-004	Two carriers of an autosomal recessive disease have an unaffected child. What is the probability that this child is a carrier?<br><br><br>A) 2/3<br>B) 1/2<br>C) 1/3<br>D) 3/4<br>E) 1/4	<b>A) 2/3</b><br><br>Offspring of Aa × Aa: 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa. Knowing the child is unaffected removes aa, leaving 3 genotypes in the ratio 1 AA : 2 Aa. So <i>P(carrier) = 2/3</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio22-006	In a population in equilibrium, 16% of people show a recessive trait. What percentage are heterozygous carriers?<br><br><br>A) 24%<br>B) 40%<br>C) 60%<br>D) 36%<br>E) 48%	<b>E) 48%</b><br><br><i>q<sup>2</sup> = 0.16</i>, so <i>q = 0.4</i> and <i>p = 0.6</i>. Carriers <i>= 2pq = 2 × 0.6 × 0.4 = 0.48</i>, which is 48%.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio22-008	A population has 900 people with allele frequency <i>q = 0.1</i> for a recessive allele. 100 immigrants with <i>q = 0.5</i> join and mate at random. What is the new value of <i>q</i>?<br><br><br>A) 0.30<br>B) 0.10<br>C) 0.50<br>D) 0.14<br>E) 0.06	<b>D) 0.14</b><br><br>The new frequency is the weighted average: <i>(900 × 0.1 + 100 × 0.5)/(1000) = (90 + 50)/(1000) = 0.14</i>. This is gene flow, one of the factors that cause a population to deviate from Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium.	ImatPath Biology practice
