chem11-016	Key points: <b>Reactivity</b>	• A <b>more reactive metal displaces a less reactive one</b> from its compounds.<br>• Order: K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al, Zn, Fe, Pb, <b>H</b>, Cu, Ag, Au.<br>• Metals above H react with dilute acid to give salt + H<sub>2</sub>.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem11-017	Key points: <b>Reactions in solution</b>	• Double displacement needs a <b>precipitate, gas or water</b>.<br>• <b>Spectator ions</b> appear unchanged on both sides and are left out of the ionic equation.<br>• Neutralisation: H<sup>+</sup> + OH<sup>-</sup> → H<sub>2</sub>O.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem11-018	Key points: <b>Combustion</b>	• Complete: CO<sub>2</sub> and H<sub>2</sub>O. Incomplete: CO (poisonous) or carbon.	ImatPath Chemistry summary

chem11-010	4.4 g of propane (<i>M = 44</i> g/mol) burn completely in oxygen: C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub> + 5O<sub>2</sub> → 3CO<sub>2</sub> + 4H<sub>2</sub>O. What mass of oxygen is used?<br><br><br>A) 16 g<br>B) 8.0 g<br>C) 32 g<br>D) 3.2 g<br>E) 160 g	<b>A) 16 g</b><br><br><i>n(C<sub>3</sub>H<sub>8</sub>) = 4.4/44 = 0.10</i> mol. Oxygen: <i>5 × 0.10 = 0.50</i> mol, mass <i>= 0.50 × 32 = 16</i> g. Check: reactants <i>4.4 + 16 = 20.4</i> g, and the products are 13.2 g of CO<sub>2</sub> and 7.2 g of H<sub>2</sub>O, total 20.4 g.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem11-012	Potassium chlorate decomposes on heating: 2KClO<sub>3</sub> → 2KCl + 3O<sub>2</sub>. What volume of oxygen at STP forms from 12.25 g of KClO<sub>3</sub>? (<i>M = 122.5</i> g/mol)<br><br><br>A) 2.24 L<br>B) 5.04 L<br>C) 1.12 L<br>D) 7.50 L<br>E) 3.36 L	<b>E) 3.36 L</b><br><br><i>n(KClO<sub>3</sub>) = 12.25/122.5 = 0.100</i> mol. The ratio KClO<sub>3</sub> : O<sub>2</sub> is 2 : 3, so <i>n(O<sub>2</sub>) = 0.150</i> mol. <i>V = 0.150 × 22.4 = 3.36</i> L.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem11-014	5.0 g of CaCO<sub>3</sub> (<i>M = 100</i>) are added to 50 cm<sup>3</sup> of 2.0 mol/L HCl. What volume of CO<sub>2</sub> forms at STP?<br><br><br>A) 2.24 L<br>B) 0.56 L<br>C) 2.80 L<br>D) 1.12 L<br>E) 5.60 L	<b>D) 1.12 L</b><br><br>CaCO<sub>3</sub> + 2HCl → CaCl<sub>2</sub> + H<sub>2</sub>O + CO<sub>2</sub>. <i>n(CaCO<sub>3</sub>) = 0.050</i> mol. <i>n(HCl) = 0.050 × 2.0 = 0.10</i> mol, which is exactly the 0.10 mol needed. So <i>n(CO<sub>2</sub>) = 0.050</i> mol and <i>V = 0.050 × 22.4 = 1.12</i> L.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
