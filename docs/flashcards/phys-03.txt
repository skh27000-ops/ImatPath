phys03-002	<b>Acceleration</b>: what is it?	<b>Acceleration</b> is the <b>rate of change of velocity</b>: <i>a = (Δ v)/(Δ t) = (v - u)/(t)</i>. Its unit is m/s<sup>2</sup>. It is a vector.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys03-017	<small>Uniformly accelerated motion</small><br><b>displacement-time</b><br><i>Slope gives · Area under it gives?</i>	<b>Slope gives:</b> velocity<br><b>Area under it gives:</b> nothing useful	ImatPath Physics recall
phys03-018	<small>Uniformly accelerated motion</small><br><b>velocity-time</b><br><i>Slope gives · Area under it gives?</i>	<b>Slope gives:</b> <b>acceleration</b><br><b>Area under it gives:</b> <b>displacement</b>	ImatPath Physics recall
phys03-019	<small>Uniformly accelerated motion</small><br><b>acceleration-time</b><br><i>Slope gives · Area under it gives?</i>	<b>Slope gives:</b> rate of change of <i>a</i><br><b>Area under it gives:</b> <b>change in velocity</b>	ImatPath Physics recall
phys03-020	<small>Projectile motion</small><br><b>Horizontal</b>	no force, so <b>constant velocity</b>. <i>x = v<sub>x</sub> t</i>.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys03-021	<small>Projectile motion</small><br><b>Vertical</b>	constant acceleration <i>g</i> downward.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys03-001	Formula to know: <b>Speed and velocity</b>	<i>average speed = (distance)/(time) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; v̄ = (Δ s)/(Δ t)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys03-003	Formula to know: <b>The four equations</b>	<i>v = u + at</i><br><i>s = ut + (1)/(2)at<sup>2</sup></i><br><i>v<sup>2</sup> = u<sup>2</sup> + 2as</i><br><i>s = ((u + v))/(2)  t</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys03-004	Formula to know: <b>Falling under gravity</b>	<i>g = 9.8 m/s<sup>2</sup></i> (often rounded to 10 m/s<sup>2</sup> in exam questions)	ImatPath Physics formula
phys03-005	Formula to know: <b>Two independent motions</b>	<i>v<sub>x</sub> = vcosθ &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; v<sub>y</sub> = vsinθ</i><br>time of flight <i>= (2vsinθ)/(g) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</i> range <i>= (v<sup>2</sup>sin 2θ)/(g)</i> (level ground)	ImatPath Physics formula
phys03-014	Key points: <b>Definitions</b>	• Velocity <i>= Δ s/Δ t</i> (vector). Acceleration <i>= Δ v/Δ t</i> (vector).<br>• Slope of <i>s</i>-<i>t</i> = velocity. Slope of <i>v</i>-<i>t</i> = acceleration. <b>Area under <i>v</i>-<i>t</i> = displacement.</b>	ImatPath Physics summary
phys03-015	Key points: <b>Equations (constant <i>a</i>)</b>	• <i>v = u + at</i><br>• <i>s = ut + (1)/(2)at<sup>2</sup></i><br>• <i>v<sup>2</sup> = u<sup>2</sup> + 2as</i><br>• <i>s = (1)/(2)(u + v)t</i>	ImatPath Physics summary
phys03-016	Key points: <b>Free fall and projectiles</b>	• Free fall: <i>a = g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup> downward, independent of mass.<br>• Projectile: horizontal <b>constant velocity</b>, vertical <b>constant acceleration <i>g</i></b>.<br>• Range is greatest at 45°. At the top, <i>v<sub>y</sub> = 0</i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys03-006	Car A moves at a constant 25 m/s. As it passes a point, car B starts from rest there and accelerates at 2.5 m/s<sup>2</sup>. How far from the point does B catch A?<br><br><br>A) 250 m<br>B) 500 m<br>C) 125 m<br>D) 1000 m<br>E) 312 m	<b>B) 500 m</b><br><br>They meet when <i>25t = (1)/(2)(2.5)t<sup>2</sup></i>, so <i>t = 2 × 25/2.5 = 20</i> s. Distance <i>= 25 × 20 = 500</i> m.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys03-008	A stone is thrown upward at 15 m/s from the top of a 40 m cliff (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>). How long does it take to reach the ground at the bottom of the cliff?<br><br><br>A) 4.77 s<br>B) 3.06 s<br>C) 2.86 s<br>D) 6.3 s<br>E) 1.71 s	<b>A) 4.77 s</b><br><br>Take up as positive: <i>-40 = 15t - 4.9t<sup>2</sup></i>, so <i>4.9t<sup>2</sup> - 15t - 40 = 0</i>. The positive root is <i>t = (15 + √(225 + 784))/(9.8) = 4.77</i> s.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys03-010	A ball is kicked at 25 m/s at <i>40<sup>°</sup></i> above the horizontal (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>). At what height is it when it passes a wall 30 m away, horizontally?<br><br><br>A) 25.2 m<br>B) 6.57 m<br>C) 12.2 m<br>D) 0.527 m<br>E) 13.1 m	<b>E) 13.1 m</b><br><br>Time to reach 30 m: <i>t = 30/(25cos 40<sup>°</sup>) = 1.57</i> s. Height <i>y = (25sin 40<sup>°</sup>)t - 4.9t<sup>2</sup> = 25.2 - 12 = 13.1</i> m.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys03-012	A stone is dropped from the top of an 80 m tower. One second later a second stone is thrown straight down so that both hit the ground together (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>). What is the initial speed of the second stone?<br><br><br>A) 9.8 m/s<br>B) 39.6 m/s<br>C) 26.3 m/s<br>D) 11.4 m/s<br>E) 5.71 m/s	<b>D) 11.4 m/s</b><br><br>First stone: <i>t = √(160/9.8) = 4.04</i> s. The second falls for <i>3.04</i> s: <i>80 = 3.04u + 4.9(3.04)<sup>2</sup></i>, so <i>u = (80 - 45.3)/3.04 = 11.4</i> m/s.	ImatPath Physics practice
