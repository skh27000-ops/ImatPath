bio02-029	<small>Eukaryotic cells</small><br><b>Age</b><br><i>Prokaryotic cell · Eukaryotic cell?</i>	<b>Prokaryotic cell:</b> About 3.5 billion years<br><b>Eukaryotic cell:</b> About 1.5 to 2 billion years	ImatPath Biology recall

bio02-035	<small>Eukaryotic cells</small><br><b>Energy store</b><br><i>Animal cell · Plant cell?</i>	<b>Animal cell:</b> <b>Glycogen</b><br><b>Plant cell:</b> <b>Starch</b>	ImatPath Biology recall
bio02-009	Key points: <b>Two kinds of cell</b>	• All cells have a membrane, cytoplasm, DNA and ribosomes.<br>• Prokaryotes: <b>no nucleus, no membrane-bound organelles</b>. Eukaryotes: nucleus and organelles.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio02-010	Key points: <b>Bacteria</b>	• Peptidoglycan wall, circular naked DNA in a nucleoid, <b>70S</b> ribosomes, plasmids.<br>• Gram-positive = thick wall, purple. Gram-negative = outer membrane, pink-red, more resistant.<br>• Divide by <b>binary fission</b>.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio02-011	Key points: <b>Animal vs plant</b>	• Plant only: <b>cellulose wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole</b>. Animal: centrioles, glycogen.<br>• Both have mitochondria.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio02-012	Key points: <b>Origin</b>	• Endosymbiosis: mitochondria and chloroplasts came from engulfed bacteria. Evidence: own DNA, 70S ribosomes, double membrane.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio02-001	After the Gram stain a bacterium appears pink. What does this tell you?<br><br><br>A) It has a thick peptidoglycan wall (Gram-positive)<br>B) It has a thin peptidoglycan wall and an outer membrane, so it did not keep the violet dye (Gram-negative)<br>C) It has no cell wall at all<br>D) It is a eukaryote<br>E) It has a nucleus	<b>B) It has a thin peptidoglycan wall and an outer membrane, so it did not keep the violet dye (Gram-negative)</b><br><br>Gram-positive bacteria have a thick peptidoglycan layer that holds the crystal violet (purple). Gram-negative bacteria have a thin layer and an outer membrane, lose the violet during decolourising and take up the pink counterstain.	ImatPath Biology practice

Mitochondria, nucleus, ribosomes, ER and Golgi are found in both.	ImatPath Biology practice

bio02-007	A cell is found to contain abundant rough ER, a large Golgi apparatus and many vesicles near the membrane. What is its most likely function?<br><br><br>A) Contracting to produce movement<br>B) Storing fat<br>C) Detoxifying drugs<br>D) Making and secreting proteins, such as digestive enzymes<br>E) Absorbing light for photosynthesis	<b>D) Making and secreting proteins, such as digestive enzymes</b><br><br>Rough ER makes proteins, the Golgi modifies and packages them, and vesicles carry them to the membrane for secretion.
