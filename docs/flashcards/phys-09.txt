phys09-001	<b>Density</b>: what is it?	<b>Density</b> is the <b>mass per unit volume</b>: <i>ρ = (m)/(V)</i>. The SI unit is kg/m<sup>3</sup>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys09-002	<b>Pressure</b>: what is it?	<b>Pressure</b> is the <b>force acting perpendicular to a surface per unit area</b>: <i>p = (F)/(A)</i>. The SI unit is the <b>pascal (Pa)</b>: <i>1 Pa = 1 N/m<sup>2</sup></i>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys09-004	<b>Pascal's principle</b>: what is it?	A change in pressure applied to an <b>enclosed fluid</b> is transmitted <b>undiminished</b> to every point of the fluid and to the walls of its container.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys09-006	<b>Archimedes' principle</b>: what is it?	The buoyant force on a body immersed in a fluid equals the <b>weight of the fluid it displaces</b>: <i>B = ρ<sub>fluid</sub>  g  V<sub>displaced</sub></i>.	ImatPath Physics definition

phys09-016	Key points: <b>Pressure in fluids</b>	• <b>Stevin</b>: <i>p = p<sub>0</sub> + ρ gh</i>. Same depth, same pressure. Shape and width do not matter.<br>• Gauge pressure <i>= ρ gh</i>. Absolute = atmospheric + gauge.<br>• <b>Pascal</b>: pressure in an enclosed fluid is transmitted undiminished: <i>F<sub>1</sub>/A<sub>1</sub> = F<sub>2</sub>/A<sub>2</sub></i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys09-017	Key points: <b>Archimedes</b>	• Buoyant force <i>B = ρ<sub>fluid</sub> g V<sub>displaced</sub></i> = weight of fluid displaced.<br>• Floats if <i>ρ<sub>object</sub> < ρ<sub>fluid</sub></i>. Submerged fraction <i>= ρ<sub>object</sub>/ρ<sub>fluid</sub></i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys09-007	A bubble of air leaves a diver at a depth of 25 m in sea water (<i>ρ = 1030</i> kg/m<sup>3</sup>) and rises to the surface at constant temperature (<i>p<sub>atm</sub> = 1.01 × 10<sup>5</sup></i> Pa, <i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>). By what factor does its volume increase?<br><br><br>A) 2.5<br>B) 3.5<br>C) 7.0<br>D) 4.5<br>E) 1.7	<b>B) 3.5</b><br><br>At 25 m: <i>p = 1.01 × 10<sup>5</sup> + 1030(9.8)(25) = 3.53 × 10<sup>5</sup></i> Pa. Boyle's law: <i>V<sub>2</sub>/V<sub>1</sub> = p<sub>1</sub>/p<sub>2</sub> = 3.53 × 10<sup>5</sup>/1.01 × 10<sup>5</sup> = 3.5</i>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys09-009	Ice has a density of 917 kg/m<sup>3</sup> and sea water 1025 kg/m<sup>3</sup>. What fraction of an iceberg's volume is above the surface?<br><br><br>A) 10.5%<br>B) 89.5%<br>C) 11.8%<br>D) 50.0%<br>E) 1.05%	<b>A) 10.5%</b><br><br>A floating body displaces its own weight: the submerged fraction is <i>917/1025 = 89.5%</i>. So the fraction above the surface is <i>1 - 0.895 = 10.5%</i>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys09-011	A metal object weighs 8.0 N in air and 6.8 N when fully submerged in water. What is the density of the metal? (water <i>ρ = 1000</i> kg/m<sup>3</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 5670 kg/m<sup>3</sup><br>B) 1180 kg/m<sup>3</sup><br>C) 150 kg/m<sup>3</sup><br>D) 13300 kg/m<sup>3</sup><br>E) 6670 kg/m<sup>3</sup>	<b>E) 6670 kg/m<sup>3</sup></b><br><br>Buoyant force <i>= 8.0 - 6.8 = 1.2</i> N <i>= ρ<sub>w</sub> g V</i>. The weight is <i>ρ<sub>m</sub> g V</i>. So <i>ρ<sub>m</sub>/ρ<sub>w</sub> = 8.0/1.2</i> and <i>ρ<sub>m</sub> = 6670</i> kg/m<sup>3</sup>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys09-013	A rectangular dam wall is 100 m wide and holds back water to a depth of 12 m. What is the total force of the water on the wall? (<i>ρ = 1000</i> kg/m<sup>3</sup>, <i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 1.41 × 10<sup>8</sup> N<br>B) 1.18 × 10<sup>7</sup> N<br>C) 3.53 × 10<sup>7</sup> N<br>D) 7.06 × 10<sup>7</sup> N<br>E) 1.18 × 10<sup>5</sup> N	<b>D) 7.06 × 10<sup>7</sup> N</b><br><br>The pressure rises linearly from 0 to <i>ρ gh</i>, so the average is <i>(1)/(2)ρ gh = 58 800</i> Pa. The wall area is <i>12 × 100 = 1200</i> m<sup>2</sup>. <i>F = 58 800 × 1200 = 7.06 × 10<sup>7</sup></i> N.	ImatPath Physics practice
