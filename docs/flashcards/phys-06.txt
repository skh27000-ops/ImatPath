phys06-001	<b>Momentum</b>: what is it?	The <b>momentum</b> of a body is its <b>mass times its velocity</b>: <i>p = mv</i>. It is a <b>vector</b>, measured in kg m/s (or N s).	ImatPath Physics definition
phys06-003	<b>Impulse</b>: what is it?	<b>Impulse</b> is the <b>change in momentum</b>, equal to the <b>force multiplied by the time</b> it acts: <i>J = FΔ t = Δp = mv - mu</i>. The unit is N s.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys06-004	<b>Conservation of linear momentum</b>: what is it?	In a <b>closed system</b> (no external resultant force), the <b>total momentum before an interaction equals the total momentum after it</b>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys06-018	<small>Types of collision</small><br><b>Elastic</b><br><i>Momentum · Kinetic energy · Example?</i>	<b>Momentum:</b> conserved<br><b>Kinetic energy:</b> <b>conserved</b><br><b>Example:</b> collision of gas molecules, billiard balls (nearly)	ImatPath Physics recall
phys06-019	<small>Types of collision</small><br><b>Inelastic</b><br><i>Momentum · Kinetic energy · Example?</i>	<b>Momentum:</b> conserved<br><b>Kinetic energy:</b> some <b>lost</b> (as heat, sound, deformation)<br><b>Example:</b> most real collisions	ImatPath Physics recall
phys06-020	<small>Types of collision</small><br><b>Perfectly inelastic</b><br><i>Momentum · Kinetic energy · Example?</i>	<b>Momentum:</b> conserved<br><b>Kinetic energy:</b> <b>maximum loss</b>, bodies move together<br><b>Example:</b> a bullet stuck in a block, a car crash	ImatPath Physics recall
phys06-021	<small>Answers & explanations</small><br><b>Elastic</b>	momentum <b>and</b> KE conserved. Approach speed = separation speed.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys06-022	<small>Answers & explanations</small><br><b>Inelastic</b>	KE lost. <b>Perfectly inelastic</b>: bodies stick together.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys06-002	Formula to know: <b>Momentum</b>	<i>F<sub>net</sub> = (Δp)/(Δ t)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys06-005	Formula to know: <b>The principle</b>	<i>m<sub>1</sub>u<sub>1</sub> + m<sub>2</sub>u<sub>2</sub> = m<sub>1</sub>v<sub>1</sub> + m<sub>2</sub>v<sub>2</sub></i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys06-006	Formula to know: <b>Where an object balances</b>	<i>x<sub>cm</sub> = (m<sub>1</sub>x<sub>1</sub> + m<sub>2</sub>x<sub>2</sub> + …)/(m<sub>1</sub> + m<sub>2</sub> + …)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys06-015	Key points: <b>Definitions</b>	• Momentum <i>p = mv</i> (vector, kg m/s).<br>• Impulse <i>J = FΔ t = Δ p</i>. Area under a <i>F</i>-<i>t</i> graph.<br>• <i>F<sub>net</sub> = Δ p/Δ t</i>. Longer contact time means a smaller force.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys06-016	Key points: <b>Conservation</b>	• Closed system: <b>total momentum is constant</b>, in every collision and explosion.<br>• Use signs for direction. In 2D, conserve each direction separately.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys06-017	Key points: <b>Collisions</b>	• <b>Elastic</b>: momentum <b>and</b> KE conserved. Approach speed = separation speed.<br>• <b>Inelastic</b>: KE lost. <b>Perfectly inelastic</b>: bodies stick together.<br>• Centre of mass <i>x<sub>cm</sub> = ∑ m x/∑ m</i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys06-007	A 20 g bullet moving at 400 m/s embeds itself in a 2.0 kg block hanging from a long string. To what height does the block swing? (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 8160 m<br>B) 0.8 m<br>C) 0.816 m<br>D) 3.96 m<br>E) 1.6 m	<b>B) 0.8 m</b><br><br>Momentum: <i>0.020 × 400 = 2.02 v</i>, so <i>v = 3.96</i> m/s. Energy: <i>h = v<sup>2</sup>/2g = 15.7/19.6 = 0.8</i> m.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys06-009	A 60 kg skater at rest on frictionless ice throws a 2.0 kg ball horizontally at 8.0 m/s (relative to the ice). What is the skater's recoil speed?<br><br><br>A) 0.267 m/s<br>B) 0.276 m/s<br>C) 8 m/s<br>D) 3.75 m/s<br>E) 16 m/s	<b>A) 0.267 m/s</b><br><br>The total momentum stays zero: <i>60v = 2.0 × 8.0</i>, so <i>v = 16/60 = 0.267</i> m/s in the opposite direction.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys06-011	A 0.15 kg ball hits a wall at 20 m/s, with its velocity at 60° to the normal to the wall, and rebounds elastically. The contact lasts 5.0 ms. What is the average force on the ball?<br><br><br>A) 1200 N<br>B) 300 N<br>C) 1040 N<br>D) 3000 N<br>E) 600 N	<b>E) 600 N</b><br><br>Only the component along the normal changes: <i>20cos 60<sup>°</sup> = 10</i> m/s, which reverses. <i>Δ p = 2(0.15)(10) = 3.0</i> N s. <i>F = 3.0/0.0050 = 600</i> N.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys06-013	A railway wagon of 8000 kg rolls at 1.5 m/s. A load of 2000 kg of sand falls vertically onto it. What is the final speed, and how much kinetic energy is lost?<br><br><br>A) 1.5 m/s and 0 J<br>B) 1.2 m/s and 0 J<br>C) 1.9 m/s and 1800 J<br>D) 1.2 m/s and 1800 J<br>E) 1.2 m/s and 9000 J	<b>D) 1.2 m/s and 1800 J</b><br><br>Horizontal momentum is conserved: <i>8000 × 1.5 = 10 000v</i>, so <i>v = 1.2</i> m/s. <i>E<sub>k</sub></i> before: <i>(1)/(2)(8000)(1.5)<sup>2</sup> = 9000</i> J. After: <i>(1)/(2)(10 000)(1.2)<sup>2</sup> = 7200</i> J. Lost: 1800 J.	ImatPath Physics practice
