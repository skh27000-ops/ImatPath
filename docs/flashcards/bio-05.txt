bio05-009	Key points: <b>Nucleus and protein factory</b>	• Nucleus: DNA, double envelope with pores, <b>nucleolus</b> makes ribosomes.<br>• Rough ER (ribosomes) → Golgi (modify, sort, package) → vesicles → membrane.<br>• Smooth ER: lipids, detoxification, calcium store.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio05-010	Key points: <b>Energy and recycling</b>	• <b>Mitochondria</b>: respiration, ATP, cristae, own DNA. <b>Chloroplasts</b>: photosynthesis, thylakoids, own DNA.<br>• <b>Lysosomes</b>: digestion. <b>Peroxisomes</b>: break down H<sub>2</sub>O<sub>2</sub> with catalase.<br>• Plant central vacuole: water store, <b>turgor</b>.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio05-011	Key points: <b>Cytoskeleton</b>	• <b>Microfilaments</b> (actin), <b>intermediate filaments</b>, <b>microtubules</b> (tubulin).<br>• Cilia and eukaryotic flagella: microtubules in a 9+2 pattern.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio05-001	Which sequence describes the path of a secreted protein, such as insulin?<br><br><br>A) Nucleus, Golgi apparatus, rough ER, lysosome, plasma membrane<br>B) Ribosome on rough ER, ER lumen, transport vesicle, Golgi apparatus, secretory vesicle, plasma membrane<br>C) Free ribosome, cytosol, nucleus, plasma membrane<br>D) Rough ER, lysosome, Golgi apparatus, mitochondrion<br>E) Golgi apparatus, rough ER, secretory vesicle, nucleus	<b>B) Ribosome on rough ER, ER lumen, transport vesicle, Golgi apparatus, secretory vesicle, plasma membrane</b><br><br>The protein is made on a ribosome attached to the rough ER and enters the ER lumen. A vesicle takes it to the Golgi apparatus, which modifies and sorts it. A secretory vesicle then fuses with the plasma membrane (exocytosis).	ImatPath Biology practice
bio05-003	Which pairing of cell type and organelle is <b>most</b> likely to be correct?<br><br><br>A) Liver cell detoxifying drugs: abundant smooth ER<br>B) Muscle cell: very few mitochondria<br>C) Pancreatic enzyme cell: abundant smooth ER but no rough ER<br>D) Macrophage that engulfs bacteria: few lysosomes<br>E) Red blood cell: large nucleus and many ribosomes	<b>A) Liver cell detoxifying drugs: abundant smooth ER</b><br><br>Smooth ER contains enzymes that detoxify drugs. Muscle cells need many mitochondria, enzyme-secreting cells have a lot of rough ER, macrophages have many lysosomes, and mature red blood cells have no nucleus.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio05-005	Colchicine prevents microtubules from assembling. What happens to dividing cells treated with it?<br><br><br>A) DNA replication stops in S phase<br>B) The nuclear envelope never forms again<br>C) Cytokinesis occurs but mitosis does not<br>D) Chromosomes cannot condense<br>E) The spindle cannot form, so chromosomes cannot be separated and cells arrest in metaphase	<b>E) The spindle cannot form, so chromosomes cannot be separated and cells arrest in metaphase</b><br><br>The spindle is made of microtubules. Without it, chromosomes line up but cannot be pulled apart, so cells stay in metaphase. This is why colchicine is used to collect cells for karyotypes.	ImatPath Biology practice
