bio15-009	Key points: <b>Asexual vs sexual</b>	• Asexual: one parent, <b>mitosis</b>, <b>clones</b> (fission, budding, fragmentation, vegetative propagation, spores, parthenogenesis).<br>• Sexual: two gametes (<b>meiosis</b>) + fertilisation → variation.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio15-010	Key points: <b>Life cycles</b>	• Humans: diploid adult, haploid gametes. Fungi: haploid adult. Plants: <b>alternation of generations</b> (sporophyte 2n → spores by meiosis → gametophyte n → gametes by mitosis).	ImatPath Biology summary
bio15-011	Key points: <b>Gametes</b>	• Spermatogenesis: 4 sperm per meiosis. Oogenesis: <b>1 egg</b> + polar bodies, meiosis II completes only at fertilisation.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio15-001	100 primary spermatocytes and 100 primary oocytes complete meiosis. How many mature gametes (sperm and eggs together) are produced?<br><br><br>A) 200<br>B) 500<br>C) 400<br>D) 800<br>E) 1200	<b>B) 500</b><br><br>Each primary spermatocyte makes 4 sperm: 400 sperm. Each primary oocyte makes 1 egg (plus polar bodies that degenerate): 100 eggs. Total <i>= 400 + 100 = 500</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio15-003	In an organism with a zygotic life cycle, which is the only diploid stage?<br><br><br>A) The zygote<br>B) The adult<br>C) The gametes<br>D) The spores<br>E) All the cells	<b>A) The zygote</b><br><br>In a zygotic cycle (many fungi and algae) the adult is haploid. The zygote is diploid and undergoes meiosis at once, giving haploid cells that grow by mitosis.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio15-005	At which stage is a human egg arrested when it is released at ovulation, and when does it complete meiosis?<br><br><br>A) Prophase I; at birth<br>B) Metaphase I; at puberty<br>C) Anaphase II; at ovulation<br>D) Telophase I; at fertilisation<br>E) Metaphase II; only if a sperm penetrates it	<b>E) Metaphase II; only if a sperm penetrates it</b><br><br>The primary oocyte is arrested in prophase I from before birth. At ovulation it has completed meiosis I and stops in metaphase II. Meiosis II is finished only when fertilisation occurs.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio15-007	Which of these is <b>not</b> a form of asexual reproduction?<br><br><br>A) Budding in yeast<br>B) Runners in strawberries<br>C) Binary fission in bacteria<br>D) Self-pollination of a flower<br>E) Fragmentation in a starfish	<b>D) Self-pollination of a flower</b><br><br>Self-pollination still involves meiosis and fertilisation of gametes, so it is sexual (although offspring can be less varied). The other four produce genetically identical offspring by mitosis.	ImatPath Biology practice
