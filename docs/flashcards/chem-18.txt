chem18-010	Key points: <b>Carbon</b>	• Four bonds, chains and rings (catenation), single, double and triple bonds.<br>• 4 single bonds: tetrahedral 109.5° (sp<sup>3</sup>). Double bond: flat 120° (sp<sup>2</sup>). Triple bond: linear 180° (sp).	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem18-011	Key points: <b>Formulas</b>	• Molecular (atoms), condensed (groups), displayed (all bonds), skeletal (corners are C, H hidden).<br>• Every carbon must have <b>four bonds</b>.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem18-012	Key points: <b>Homologous series</b>	• Same functional group, same general formula, differ by CH<sub>2</sub>, similar chemistry, gradual physical change.<br>• Alkanes C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n+2</sub>. Alkenes C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n</sub>. Alkynes C<sub>n</sub>H<sub>2n-2</sub>.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem18-013	Key points: <b>Naming</b>	• Stems: meth, eth, prop, but, pent, hex, hept, oct, non, dec.<br>• Longest chain, lowest numbers, branches alphabetical, di/tri for repeats.<br>• -ane, -ene, -yne, -ol. Halogens: chloro, bromo.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem18-002	How many sigma (σ) and pi (π) bonds are in acrylonitrile, CH<sub>2</sub>=CH–C≡N?<br><br><br>A) 6 σ and 4 π<br>B) 6 σ and 3 π<br>C) 5 σ and 3 π<br>D) 7 σ and 2 π<br>E) 8 σ and 1 π	<b>B) 6 σ and 3 π</b><br><br>σ bonds: C=C (1), C–C (1), C≡N (1), three C–H (3) <i>= 6</i>. π bonds: one in C=C and two in C≡N <i>= 3</i>. A double bond is one σ plus one π, a triple bond is one σ plus two π.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem18-004	What is the IUPAC name of (CH<sub>3</sub>)<sub>2</sub>CHCH<sub>2</sub>CH(CH<sub>3</sub>)CH<sub>2</sub>CH<sub>3</sub>?<br><br><br>A) 2,4-dimethylhexane<br>B) 3,5-dimethylhexane<br>C) 2,4,4-trimethylpentane<br>D) 2-ethyl-4-methylpentane<br>E) 2,4-dimethylheptane	<b>A) 2,4-dimethylhexane</b><br><br>The longest chain has 6 carbons (hexane): CH<sub>3</sub>–CH(CH<sub>3</sub>)–CH<sub>2</sub>–CH(CH<sub>3</sub>)–CH<sub>2</sub>–CH<sub>3</sub>. Numbering from the left puts the methyl groups on carbons 2 and 4 (from the right they would be 3 and 5, which is a higher set).	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem18-006	In propyne, CH<sub>3</sub>–C≡C–H, how many atoms lie on one straight line?<br><br><br>A) 2<br>B) 3<br>C) 5<br>D) 6<br>E) 4	<b>E) 4</b><br><br>A triple bond makes the two carbons and the two atoms attached to them linear: the CH<sub>3</sub> carbon, the two sp carbons and the terminal H. The three H atoms of CH<sub>3</sub> point away (tetrahedral), so they are not on the line.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem18-008	A saturated, acyclic compound has the formula C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub>O. What is the formula of the corresponding aldehyde or ketone with four carbon atoms?<br><br><br>A) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub>O<sub>2</sub><br>B) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>6</sub>O<br>C) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub>O<br>D) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>O<br>E) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>O<sub>2</sub>	<b>D) C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>O</b><br><br>Oxidising an alcohol to a carbonyl compound removes 2 H. A C=O double bond adds one degree of unsaturation, so C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>10</sub>O becomes C<sub>4</sub>H<sub>8</sub>O (butanal or butanone).	ImatPath Chemistry practice
