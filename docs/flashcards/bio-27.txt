bio27-022	<b>species</b>: what is it?	The <b>biological species concept</b> defines a <b>species</b> as a group of populations whose members <b>can interbreed in nature and produce fertile offspring</b>, and are reproductively isolated from other groups.	ImatPath Biology term
bio27-012	<small>Theories of evolution</small><br><b>Inheritance of acquired characteristics</b><br><i>Proposed by · Content · Verdict?</i>	<b>Proposed by:</b> <b>Lamarck</b> (1809)<br><b>Content:</b> Use or disuse changes an organism and the change is passed on<br><b>Verdict:</b> <b>Wrong</b>: acquired changes are not inherited	ImatPath Biology recall
bio27-013	<small>Theories of evolution</small><br><b>Natural selection</b><br><i>Proposed by · Content · Verdict?</i>	<b>Proposed by:</b> <b>Darwin and Wallace</b> (1858–59)<br><b>Content:</b> Variation + inheritance + struggle → better-adapted individuals leave more offspring<br><b>Verdict:</b> <b>Correct</b>, but Darwin did not know how inheritance works	ImatPath Biology recall
bio27-014	<small>Theories of evolution</small><br><b>Modern synthesis (1930s–40s)</b><br><i>Proposed by · Content · Verdict?</i>	<b>Proposed by:</b> Fisher, Haldane, Wright, Dobzhansky and others<br><b>Content:</b> Combines Darwin's selection with <b>Mendelian genetics</b>: variation comes from <b>mutation and recombination</b>, and evolution is a <b>change in allele frequencies</b> in a population<br><b>Verdict:</b> Basis of present theory	ImatPath Biology recall

bio27-023	<small>Evidence for evolution</small><br><b>Fossil record</b>	fossils show <b>change over time</b> in layers of rock, with <b>transitional forms</b> such as <b>Tiktaalik</b> (fish-to-tetrapod), <b>Archaeopteryx</b> (reptile-bird features) and whale ancestors with legs. Older fossils are deeper, and their ages can be measured by <b>radiometric dating</b>.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-024	<small>Evidence for evolution</small><br><b>Embryology</b>	vertebrate embryos look alike in early stages and all show <b>pharyngeal pouches</b> and a tail.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-025	<small>Evidence for evolution</small><br><b>Molecular biology</b>	all life uses <b>DNA and the same genetic code</b>. The more recently two species shared an ancestor, the more <b>similar</b> their DNA and proteins are. Humans and chimpanzees differ by only about <b>1–2%</b> in DNA. <b>Cytochrome c</b> and haemoglobin sequences give the same family tree as anatomy.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-026	<small>Evidence for evolution</small><br><b>Biogeography</b>	the distribution of species. Island species resemble those of the nearest mainland. Marsupials in Australia show <b>adaptive radiation</b> from a common ancestor.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-027	<small>Species and speciation</small><br><b>Allopatric speciation</b>	a <b>geographical barrier</b> (a mountain range, a river, or colonisation of an island) <b>splits</b> a population. The groups evolve apart by selection and drift, and eventually can no longer interbreed. This is the most common mode (for example, Darwin's finches).	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-028	<small>Species and speciation</small><br><b>Sympatric speciation</b>	new species form <b>in the same area</b>, without a physical barrier. It is common in <b>plants</b>, through <b>polyploidy</b> (a doubling of chromosome sets can make instant reproductive isolation). In animals it can occur through habitat or food specialisation.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-029	<small>Answers & explanations</small><br><b>Lamarck</b>	acquired traits inherited (wrong). <b>Darwin</b>: natural selection. <b>Modern synthesis</b> = selection + genetics. Evolution = <b>change in allele frequencies</b>.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio27-009	Key points: <b>Theories</b>	• <b>Lamarck</b>: acquired traits inherited (wrong). <b>Darwin</b>: natural selection. <b>Modern synthesis</b> = selection + genetics. Evolution = <b>change in allele frequencies</b>.<br>• Causes: mutation, gene flow, drift, selection, non-random mating.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio27-010	Key points: <b>Evidence</b>	• <b>Fossils</b> (transitional forms), <b>homologous</b> vs <b>analogous</b> vs <b>vestigial</b>, embryology, <b>DNA and protein similarity</b>, biogeography, direct observation.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio27-011	Key points: <b>Species</b>	• Biological species: interbreed and make <b>fertile</b> offspring. Prezygotic and postzygotic barriers.<br>• <b>Allopatric</b> (geographic barrier), <b>sympatric</b> (same area, e.g. polyploidy). <b>Adaptive radiation</b>.<br>• Phylogenetic tree: nodes are common ancestors.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio27-001	Which statement about giraffe necks is consistent with Darwin's theory?<br><br><br>A) Giraffes stretched their necks and passed the longer necks on<br>B) Giraffes with longer necks survived and reproduced more, so the trait became more common<br>C) Giraffes needed longer necks, so they grew them<br>D) All giraffes changed in one generation<br>E) Short-necked giraffes became long-necked during their lives	<b>B) Giraffes with longer necks survived and reproduced more, so the trait became more common</b><br><br>Darwin proposed variation among individuals, inheritance and differential survival and reproduction. Lamarck proposed that traits acquired during life are inherited, which is not supported by evidence.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio27-003	Two species differ by 12 neutral nucleotide substitutions. Each lineage accumulates 1 substitution per 2 million years. How long ago did they diverge?<br><br><br>A) 12 million years<br>B) 6 million years<br>C) 24 million years<br>D) 2 million years<br>E) 48 million years	<b>A) 12 million years</b><br><br>Substitutions accumulate in <b>both</b> lineages after the split. The total rate is <i>2 × 1/2 = 1</i> substitution per million years. <i>12/1 = 12</i> million years.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio27-005	Two plant species each have <i>2n = 14</i>. They hybridise and the sterile hybrid doubles its chromosome number by an error in meiosis, becoming fertile. How many chromosomes does the new species have?<br><br><br>A) 14<br>B) 7<br>C) 21<br>D) 56<br>E) 28	<b>E) 28</b><br><br>The hybrid has 14 chromosomes (7 from each parent), which cannot pair properly. Doubling gives 28, so each chromosome has a partner and meiosis works. This is a sympatric speciation by allopolyploidy.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio27-007	Sequence differences are: A–B 2, A–C 10, B–C 10. Which statement is correct?<br><br><br>A) A and C are the closest relatives<br>B) B and C are the closest relatives<br>C) All three diverged at the same time<br>D) A and B share a more recent common ancestor with each other than with C<br>E) C is the ancestor of A and B	<b>D) A and B share a more recent common ancestor with each other than with C</b><br><br>The smaller the difference, the more recent the split. A and B differ only slightly, so they form a branch (clade) that split from C earlier.	ImatPath Biology practice
