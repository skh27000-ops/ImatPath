phys01-003	Formula to know: <b>Significant figures</b>	Multiplying or dividing: keep as many significant figures as the <b>least precise</b> number.<br>Adding or subtracting: keep as many <b>decimal places</b> as the least precise number.	ImatPath Physics formula
phys01-004	Formula to know: <b>Uncertainty and percentage error</b>	<i>percentage uncertainty = (Δ x)/(x) × 100%</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys01-013	Key points: <b>Units</b>	• Physical quantity = <b>number × unit</b>.<br>• SI base units: <b>m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd</b>.<br>• Derived: N = kg m s<sup>-2</sup>, J = N m, W = J/s, Pa = N/m<sup>2</sup>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys01-014	Key points: <b>Prefixes and conversion</b>	• T 10<sup>12</sup>, G 10<sup>9</sup>, M 10<sup>6</sup>, k 10<sup>3</sup>, m 10<sup>-3</sup>, µ 10<sup>-6</sup>, n 10<sup>-9</sup>, p 10<sup>-12</sup>.<br>• Multiply by a fraction equal to 1. 1 m<sup>2</sup> = 10<sup>4</sup> cm<sup>2</sup>, 1 m<sup>3</sup> = 10<sup>6</sup> cm<sup>3</sup>, 1 L = 10<sup>-3</sup> m<sup>3</sup>.<br>• km/h ÷ 3.6 = m/s.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys01-015	Key points: <b>Numbers</b>	• Scientific notation <i>a × 10<sup>n</sup></i> with <i>1 ≤ a < 10</i>.<br>• Multiply or divide: keep the <b>least significant figures</b>. Add or subtract: keep the <b>least decimal places</b>.<br>• Percentage uncertainty = <i>Δ x/x × 100%</i>. Multiplying: add percentages.<br>• Both sides of an equation must have the <b>same dimensions</b>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys01-005	A rectangular tank is 2.5 m long, 40 cm wide and 1.2 dm deep. It is completely filled with water (<i>ρ = 1.0</i> g/cm<sup>3</sup>). What is the mass of the water?<br><br><br>A) 12 kg<br>B) 120 kg<br>C) 1200 kg<br>D) 0.12 kg<br>E) 1.2 × 10<sup>5</sup> kg	<b>B) 120 kg</b><br><br>Convert to metres first: <i>2.5 × 0.40 × 0.12 = 0.12</i> m<sup>3</sup>. The density is 1000 kg/m<sup>3</sup>, so <i>m = 0.12 × 1000 = 120</i> kg.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys01-007	A plate has length <i>(12.0 ± 0.2)</i> cm and width <i>(5.0 ± 0.1)</i> cm. What is the area with its absolute uncertainty?<br><br><br>A) (60 ± 2.2) cm<sup>2</sup><br>B) (60.0 ± 0.3) cm<sup>2</sup><br>C) (60.0 ± 1.0) cm<sup>2</sup><br>D) (60.0 ± 0.02) cm<sup>2</sup><br>E) (60.0 ± 1.7) cm<sup>2</sup>	<b>A) (60 ± 2.2) cm<sup>2</sup></b><br><br>For a product, add the <b>percentage</b> uncertainties: <i>0.2/12.0 = 1.67%</i> and <i>0.1/5.0 = 2.0%</i>, total <i>3.67%</i>. Then <i>3.67% × 60.0 = 2.2</i> cm<sup>2</sup>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys01-009	The mass of a hydrogen atom is <i>1.67 × 10<sup>-27</sup></i> kg. About how many hydrogen atoms are in 1.0 g?<br><br><br>A) 5.99 × 10<sup>26</sup><br>B) 5.99 × 10<sup>20</sup><br>C) 1.67 × 10<sup>-24</sup><br>D) 1.67 × 10<sup>-30</sup><br>E) 5.99 × 10<sup>23</sup>	<b>E) 5.99 × 10<sup>23</sup></b><br><br><i>1.0 g = 1.0 × 10<sup>-3</sup></i> kg. <i>N = 10<sup>-3</sup>/(1.67 × 10<sup>-27</sup>) = 6.0 × 10<sup>23</sup></i>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys01-011	A sample has mass <i>(50.0 ± 0.5)</i> g and volume <i>(20.0 ± 0.4)</i> cm<sup>3</sup>. What is the density with its uncertainty?<br><br><br>A) (2.50 ± 0.9) g/cm<sup>3</sup><br>B) (2.50 ± 0.03) g/cm<sup>3</sup><br>C) (2.50 ± 0.5) g/cm<sup>3</sup><br>D) (2.5 ± 0.07) g/cm<sup>3</sup><br>E) (2.50 ± 0.2) g/cm<sup>3</sup>	<b>D) (2.5 ± 0.07) g/cm<sup>3</sup></b><br><br><i>ρ = 50.0/20.0 = 2.50</i> g/cm<sup>3</sup>. Percentage uncertainties: <i>0.5/50.0 = 1%</i> and <i>0.4/20.0 = 2%</i>, which add to 3%. <i>3% × 2.50 = 0.075 ≈ 0.08</i>.	ImatPath Physics practice
