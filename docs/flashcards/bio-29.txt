bio29-039	<small>Defence</small><br><b>Innate (non-specific) immunity</b>	<b>skin and mucus</b>, stomach acid, <b>phagocytes</b> (neutrophils, macrophages), <b>inflammation</b>, fever, <b>natural killer cells</b>. It reacts at once, the same way to any pathogen.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio29-040	<small>Defence</small><br><b>Adaptive (specific) immunity</b>	slower, <b>specific</b> and with <b>memory</b>. <b>B cells</b> make <b>antibodies</b> (humoral immunity). <b>T cells</b>: <b>helper T cells</b> coordinate the response, <b>cytotoxic T cells</b> kill infected cells (cell-mediated immunity). After infection, <b>memory cells</b> give faster protection in future.	ImatPath Biology key idea

bio29-010	Key points: <b>Breathing and excretion</b>	• Alveoli: huge area, thin wall. Inhalation: diaphragm contracts, volume up, pressure down.<br>• Nephron: <b>filtration</b>, <b>selective reabsorption</b>, loop of Henle, <b>ADH</b> at the collecting duct.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio29-011	Key points: <b>Control and defence</b>	• Nervous: fast, electrical. Endocrine: slower, hormones. <b>Innate</b> (immediate, non-specific) vs <b>adaptive</b> (B and T cells, memory). Vaccines give memory.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio29-012	Key points: <b>Reproduction</b>	• Menstrual cycle: FSH, oestrogen, <b>LH surge → ovulation</b>, progesterone. Placenta makes <b>hCG</b>.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio29-001	A red blood cell leaves the right ventricle. What is the correct order of the vessels and chambers it passes through before it reaches the aorta?<br><br><br>A) Vena cava, lungs, aorta, left ventricle<br>B) Pulmonary artery, lungs, pulmonary vein, left atrium, left ventricle<br>C) Pulmonary vein, lungs, pulmonary artery, left atrium<br>D) Aorta, left atrium, lungs, left ventricle<br>E) Pulmonary artery, left atrium, lungs, left ventricle	<b>B) Pulmonary artery, lungs, pulmonary vein, left atrium, left ventricle</b><br><br>Right ventricle → pulmonary artery → capillaries in the lungs → pulmonary vein → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta. This is the pulmonary circuit followed by the systemic circuit (double circulation).	ImatPath Biology practice
bio29-003	A gallstone blocks the bile duct. Which result is most likely?<br><br><br>A) Poor digestion of fats, because bile salts do not reach the intestine<br>B) No digestion of proteins<br>C) No digestion of starch in the mouth<br>D) The stomach stops making acid<br>E) Blood glucose rises sharply	<b>A) Poor digestion of fats, because bile salts do not reach the intestine</b><br><br>Bile (made in the liver, stored in the gallbladder) emulsifies fats in the small intestine, increasing the surface area for lipase. A blocked duct reduces fat digestion and absorption, and stools become pale.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio29-005	A patient has glucose in the urine. Which explanation is correct for an untreated diabetic?<br><br><br>A) The kidney makes glucose<br>B) Glucose is not filtered normally<br>C) The collecting duct secretes glucose<br>D) The bladder makes glucose<br>E) The blood glucose is so high that the filtered glucose exceeds the capacity of the transporters in the proximal tubule	<b>E) The blood glucose is so high that the filtered glucose exceeds the capacity of the transporters in the proximal tubule</b><br><br>Normally all filtered glucose is reabsorbed by carriers in the proximal tubule. When blood glucose is very high, the carriers are saturated, so some glucose stays in the filtrate and is excreted.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio29-007	Why is the second infection with the same pathogen usually milder than the first?<br><br><br>A) Antibodies from the first infection are never destroyed and make a new pathogen<br>B) The body has more neutrophils<br>C) The pathogen mutates to a weaker form<br>D) Memory B and T cells respond faster and more strongly<br>E) The skin becomes thicker	<b>D) Memory B and T cells respond faster and more strongly</b><br><br>After the first exposure some B and T cells become long-lived memory cells. On the second exposure they proliferate and make antibodies quickly, so the pathogen is cleared before symptoms appear. Vaccines work in the same way.	ImatPath Biology practice
