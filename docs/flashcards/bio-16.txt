bio16-028	<small>Probability</small><br><b>Multiplication rule</b>	the probability of <b>two independent events both</b> happening is the <b>product</b>. P(boy then girl) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4.	ImatPath Biology key idea

bio16-011	Key points: <b>Crosses</b>	• <b>Aa × Aa → 3 : 1</b> (genotypes 1 : 2 : 1). <b>Dihybrid → 9 : 3 : 3 : 1</b>.<br>• <b>Test cross</b> with the homozygous recessive.<br>• Probabilities: <b>multiply</b> for "and", <b>add</b> for "or".	ImatPath Biology summary

bio16-002	Two plants heterozygous for two independent genes (AaBb × AaBb) are crossed. What is the probability that an offspring shows the dominant trait for A and the recessive trait for B?<br><br><br>A) 9/16<br>B) 3/16<br>C) 1/16<br>D) 6/16<br>E) 3/4	<b>B) 3/16</b><br><br>P(dominant for A) <i>= 3/4</i> and P(recessive for B, bb) <i>= 1/4</i>. For independent genes multiply: <i>3/4 × 1/4 = 3/16</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio16-004	Snapdragon flower colour shows incomplete dominance: red (RR) × white (rr) gives pink (Rr). A pink plant is crossed with a white plant. What are the expected offspring?<br><br><br>A) 1/2 pink and 1/2 white<br>B) All pink<br>C) 1/2 red and 1/2 white<br>D) 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white<br>E) 3/4 pink and 1/4 white	<b>A) 1/2 pink and 1/2 white</b><br><br>Rr × rr gives Rr (pink) and rr (white) in equal numbers.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio16-006	Two carriers of albinism (Aa) have two children. What is the probability that the first is unaffected and the second is albino?<br><br><br>A) 1/16<br>B) 1/4<br>C) 9/16<br>D) 3/8<br>E) 3/16	<b>E) 3/16</b><br><br>P(unaffected) <i>= 3/4</i> and P(albino) <i>= 1/4</i>. The events are independent: <i>3/4 × 1/4 = 3/16</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio16-008	In an F2 generation of 929 pea plants from a monohybrid cross of purple (dominant) and white flowers, how many white-flowered plants would you expect?<br><br><br>A) About 697<br>B) About 464<br>C) About 310<br>D) About 232<br>E) About 929	<b>D) About 232</b><br><br>Segregation gives a 3 : 1 ratio, so 1/4 of the plants are white (homozygous recessive): <i>929/4 = 232</i>.
