bio11-002	The reaction glucose + P<sub>i</sub> → glucose 6-phosphate needs +13.8 kJ/mol. It is coupled to the hydrolysis of ATP, which releases 30.5 kJ/mol. What is the net free energy change of the coupled reaction?<br><br><br>A) +44.3 kJ/mol<br>B) −16.7 kJ/mol<br>C) −44.3 kJ/mol<br>D) +16.7 kJ/mol<br>E) −13.8 kJ/mol	<b>B) −16.7 kJ/mol</b><br><br>Add the two values: <i>+13.8 + (-30.5) = -16.7</i> kJ/mol. A negative value means the coupled reaction is spontaneous. This is how ATP drives endergonic reactions.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio11-004	Dinitrophenol makes the inner mitochondrial membrane leaky to protons. What happens to ATP production, heat production and oxygen consumption?<br><br><br>A) ATP falls, heat rises, and oxygen consumption continues or rises<br>B) ATP rises, heat falls, oxygen consumption falls<br>C) ATP falls, heat falls, oxygen consumption falls<br>D) ATP is unchanged, heat rises, oxygen consumption falls<br>E) ATP rises, heat rises, oxygen consumption rises	<b>A) ATP falls, heat rises, and oxygen consumption continues or rises</b><br><br>The proton gradient is dissipated without passing through ATP synthase, so little ATP is made. The energy is released as heat. The electron transport chain keeps running (or speeds up) trying to restore the gradient, so oxygen is still used.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio11-006	The Na<sup>+</sup>/K<sup>+</sup> pump is phosphorylated by ATP. What is the effect of attaching the phosphate group?<br><br><br>A) The protein is digested<br>B) The ions are converted into water<br>C) The membrane becomes more permeable to everything<br>D) The phosphate is used to make DNA<br>E) The protein changes shape, which moves ions across the membrane	<b>E) The protein changes shape, which moves ions across the membrane</b><br><br>Transfer of a phosphate group from ATP changes the conformation of the pump protein. This releases Na<sup>+</sup> outside and lets K<sup>+</sup> bind, so ATP energy is converted into the work of active transport.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio11-008	Which of these processes does <b>not</b> directly require ATP?<br><br><br>A) Muscle contraction<br>B) Active transport by the Na<sup>+</sup>/K<sup>+</sup> pump<br>C) Synthesis of a polypeptide (activation of amino acids)<br>D) Osmosis<br>E) Exocytosis	<b>D) Osmosis</b><br><br>Osmosis is the passive diffusion of water down its water potential gradient. The other processes use ATP (or GTP) for movement, transport, bond formation or vesicle movement.	ImatPath Biology practice
