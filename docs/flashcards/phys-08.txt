phys08-001	<b>Work</b>: what is it?	The <b>work</b> done by a constant force is the force times the displacement <b>in the direction of the force</b>: <i>W = Fdcosθ</i>, where <i>θ</i> is the angle between the force and the displacement. The unit is the <b>joule (J)</b>: <i>1 J = 1 N m</i>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys08-003	<b>Work-energy theorem</b>: what is it?	The <b>net work</b> done on an object equals its <b>change in kinetic energy</b>: <i>W<sub>net</sub> = (1)/(2)mv<sup>2</sup> - (1)/(2)mu<sup>2</sup></i>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys08-005	<b>Conservation of energy</b>: what is it?	Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It can only be <b>transferred</b> from one store to another, or from one object to another. The <b>total energy of a closed system is constant</b>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys08-006	<b>Power</b>: what is it?	<b>Power</b> is the <b>rate of doing work</b> or of transferring energy: <i>P = (W)/(t) = (Δ E)/(t)</i>. The unit is the <b>watt (W)</b>: 1 W = 1 J/s.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys08-002	Formula to know: <b>Kinetic energy and the work-energy theorem</b>	<i>E<sub>k</sub> = (1)/(2)mv<sup>2</sup></i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys08-004	Formula to know: <b>Potential energy</b>	Gravitational (near the surface): <i>E<sub>p</sub> = mgh</i><br>Elastic (spring): <i>E<sub>p</sub> = (1)/(2)kx<sup>2</sup></i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys08-007	Formula to know: <b>Power</b>	<i>P = Fv</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys08-008	Formula to know: <b>Efficiency</b>	<i>efficiency = (useful output energy (or power))/(total input energy (or power)) × 100%</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys08-017	Key points: <b>Work and energy</b>	• <i>W = Fdcosθ</i>. Area under <i>F</i>-<i>x</i> = work. Unit: joule.<br>• <i>E<sub>k</sub> = (1)/(2)mv<sup>2</sup></i>. <i>E<sub>p</sub> = mgh</i>. Spring <i>E = (1)/(2)kx<sup>2</sup></i>.<br>• Work-energy theorem: <i>W<sub>net</sub> = Δ E<sub>k</sub></i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys08-018	Key points: <b>Conservation</b>	• Total energy of a closed system is <b>constant</b>.<br>• Conservative forces (gravity, spring): mechanical energy conserved. Friction: turns it into <b>heat</b>.<br>• Falling: <i>v = √(2gh)</i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys08-019	Key points: <b>Power and efficiency</b>	• <i>P = W/t = Fv</i>. 1 W = 1 J/s. 1 kWh = <i>3.6 × 10<sup>6</sup></i> J.<br>• Efficiency = useful output / total input, always below 100%.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys08-009	A 1200 kg car climbs 30 m vertically in 20 s at a constant 15 m/s. The air and road resistance is 400 N. What power must the engine deliver? (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 17.6 kW<br>B) 23.6 kW<br>C) 6 kW<br>D) 11.6 kW<br>E) 47.3 kW	<b>B) 23.6 kW</b><br><br>Raising the car: <i>mgh/t = 1200 × 9.8 × 30/20 = 17.6</i> kW. Overcoming resistance: <i>Fv = 400 × 15 = 6.0</i> kW. Total <i>= 23.6</i> kW.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys08-011	A 2.0 kg block slides from rest down a rough slope 5.0 m long, dropping through 3.0 m of height. It reaches the bottom at 5.0 m/s. What is the average friction force? (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 6.76 N<br>B) 33.8 N<br>C) 11.3 N<br>D) 11.8 N<br>E) 5 N	<b>A) 6.76 N</b><br><br>Energy lost to friction: <i>mgh - (1)/(2)mv<sup>2</sup> = 58.8 - 25 = 33.8</i> J. This equals <i>f ×</i> (slope length): <i>f = 33.8/5.0 = 6.76</i> N.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys08-013	A pump lifts 1200 litres of water per minute through a height of 15 m. The motor takes 5.0 kW of electrical power. What is the efficiency of the system? (1 L of water has a mass of 1.0 kg, <i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 3500%<br>B) 41%<br>C) 0.98%<br>D) 100%<br>E) 59%	<b>E) 59%</b><br><br>The mass lifted per second is <i>1200/60 = 20</i> kg/s. Useful power: <i>20 × 9.8 × 15 = 2940</i> W. Efficiency <i>= 2940/5000 = 59%</i>.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys08-015	A 1500 kg car travelling at 90 km/h brakes to a stop over a distance of 50 m. What is the average braking force?<br><br><br>A) 122 kN<br>B) 4.69 kN<br>C) 0.75 kN<br>D) 9.38 kN<br>E) 33.8 kN	<b>D) 9.38 kN</b><br><br><i>90</i> km/h <i>= 25</i> m/s. Work-energy: <i>F × 50 = (1)/(2)(1500)(25)<sup>2</sup> = 468 750</i> J. <i>F = 9375</i> N <i>= 9.4</i> kN.	ImatPath Physics practice
