bio09-037	<small>Building big molecules</small><br><b>Condensation reaction</b>	two monomers join and a molecule of <b>water is released</b>. This builds polymers.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio09-038	<small>Building big molecules</small><br><b>Hydrolysis</b>	water is <b>added</b> to break a bond. This splits polymers, for example in digestion.	ImatPath Biology key idea
bio09-009	Key points: <b>Polymers</b>	• <b>Condensation</b> joins monomers and releases water. <b>Hydrolysis</b> adds water to break the bond.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio09-010	Key points: <b>Carbohydrates</b>	• Glucose → maltose, sucrose, lactose (disaccharides). Starch (plants), <b>glycogen</b> (animals), <b>cellulose</b> (β-glucose, cell wall).	ImatPath Biology summary
bio09-011	Key points: <b>Lipids</b>	• Triglyceride = glycerol + 3 fatty acids (ester bonds). Saturated vs unsaturated. Phospholipids form bilayers. Cholesterol is a steroid.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio09-012	Key points: <b>Proteins</b>	• Amino acids joined by <b>peptide bonds</b>. <b>Primary</b> sequence, <b>secondary</b> helix and sheet, <b>tertiary</b> 3-D, <b>quaternary</b> several chains.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio09-013	Key points: <b>Nucleic acids</b>	• Nucleotide = sugar + phosphate + base. DNA: deoxyribose, T, double. RNA: ribose, U, single.	ImatPath Biology summary
bio09-001	A polypeptide of 150 amino acids is made in a ribosome. How many peptide bonds and how many water molecules are produced?<br><br><br>A) 150 bonds and 150 water molecules<br>B) 149 bonds and 149 water molecules<br>C) 149 bonds and 150 water molecules<br>D) 151 bonds and 149 water molecules<br>E) 75 bonds and 75 water molecules	<b>B) 149 bonds and 149 water molecules</b><br><br>Each peptide bond is made by a condensation reaction that releases one water. Joining <i>n</i> amino acids in a chain needs <i>n - 1</i> bonds, so <i>150 - 1 = 149</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio09-003	How many water molecules are released in the formation of two triglyceride molecules from glycerol and fatty acids?<br><br><br>A) 6<br>B) 2<br>C) 3<br>D) 4<br>E) 9	<b>A) 6</b><br><br>Each triglyceride has 3 ester bonds, and each bond forms by condensation with the release of 1 water: <i>3</i> per triglyceride. For two: <i>2 × 3 = 6</i>.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio09-005	In sickle cell disease, a single amino acid (glutamic acid) is replaced by valine. Which level of protein structure is changed directly by the mutation, and which bonds stabilise an α helix?<br><br><br>A) Secondary structure; disulfide bridges<br>B) Tertiary structure; peptide bonds<br>C) Quaternary structure; ionic bonds<br>D) Primary structure; ester bonds<br>E) Primary structure; hydrogen bonds between backbone groups	<b>E) Primary structure; hydrogen bonds between backbone groups</b><br><br>The mutation changes the amino acid sequence, which is the primary structure. The α helix (secondary structure) is held by hydrogen bonds between the C=O and N–H groups of the backbone, not by side chains.	ImatPath Biology practice
bio09-007	How many of these molecules are steroids: oestrogen, testosterone, insulin, cortisol, adrenaline?<br><br><br>A) 2<br>B) 4<br>C) 5<br>D) 3<br>E) 1	<b>D) 3</b><br><br>Oestrogen, testosterone and cortisol are steroids (made from cholesterol, with four fused rings). Insulin is a protein hormone and adrenaline is derived from the amino acid tyrosine.	ImatPath Biology practice
