chem04-025	<small>Filling the orbitals</small><br><b>Aufbau principle</b>	fill the <b>lowest energy</b> orbitals first.	ImatPath Chemistry key idea
chem04-026	<small>Filling the orbitals</small><br><b>Pauli exclusion principle</b>	an orbital holds at most two electrons, and they must have <b>opposite spins</b> (shown as ↑ and ↓).	ImatPath Chemistry key idea
chem04-027	<small>Filling the orbitals</small><br><b>Hund's rule</b>	in a subshell with several orbitals, put <b>one electron in each orbital first</b>, all with the same spin, and only then pair them up.	ImatPath Chemistry key idea

chem04-013	Key points: <b>Structure</b>	• Shell <i>n</i> holds <i>2n<sup>2</sup></i> electrons: 2, 8, 18, 32.<br>• Subshells: <b>s</b> (1 orbital, 2 e), <b>p</b> (3, 6 e), <b>d</b> (5, 10 e), <b>f</b> (7, 14 e).<br>• An orbital holds at most <b>2</b> electrons, with opposite spins.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem04-014	Key points: <b>Rules</b>	• <b>Aufbau</b>: lowest energy first.<br>• <b>Pauli</b>: max two electrons per orbital, opposite spins.<br>• <b>Hund</b>: one electron per orbital first, same spin, then pair.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem04-015	Key points: <b>Writing it</b>	• Superscripts add up to <i>Z</i>. Use noble-gas shorthand: [Ne] 3s<sup>1</sup>.<br>• Valence electrons = electrons in the outer shell (group number for main groups).<br>• Positive ions: remove electrons from the <b>outer shell</b>; for transition metals <b>4s before 3d</b>.<br>• Exceptions: <b>Cr</b> [Ar] 4s<sup>1</sup> 3d<sup>5</sup>, <b>Cu</b> [Ar] 4s<sup>1</sup> 3d<sup>10</sup>.<br>• Isoelectronic: same number of electrons (Na<sup>+</sup>, F<sup>-</sup>, O<sup>2-</sup>, Ne).	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem04-005	How many unpaired electrons are in an Fe<sup>3+</sup> ion in its ground state? (Fe: <i>Z = 26</i>)<br><br><br>A) 4<br>B) 5<br>C) 3<br>D) 6<br>E) 1	<b>B) 5</b><br><br>Fe is [Ar]3d<sup>6</sup>4s<sup>2</sup>. The 4s electrons leave first, then one 3d: Fe<sup>3+</sup> is [Ar]3d<sup>5</sup>. The five d electrons occupy five orbitals singly (Hund), so 5 are unpaired.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
