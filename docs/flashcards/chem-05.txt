chem05-001	<b>Atomic radius</b>: what is it?	The <b>atomic radius</b> is half the distance between the nuclei of two bonded atoms of the same element. It measures the size of an atom.	ImatPath Chemistry definition
chem05-002	<b>First ionisation energy</b>: what is it?	The <b>first ionisation energy</b> is the energy needed to remove <b>one mole of electrons</b> from one mole of <b>gaseous atoms</b>, to form one mole of gaseous 1+ ions.<br><i>X(g) → X<sup>+</sup>(g) + e<sup>-</sup></i>	ImatPath Chemistry definition
chem05-003	<b>Electronegativity</b>: what is it?	<b>Electronegativity</b> is a measure of how strongly an atom <b>attracts the electrons in a covalent bond</b>.	ImatPath Chemistry definition

chem05-014	Key points: <b>The table</b>	• <b>Period</b> = row = number of shells. <b>Group</b> = column = valence electrons (main groups).<br>• Blocks: <b>s</b> (1, 2), <b>p</b> (13 to 18), <b>d</b> (transition), <b>f</b>.<br>• Group 1 alkali metals, group 2 alkaline earth metals, group 17 halogens, group 18 noble gases.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem05-015	Key points: <b>Trends</b>	• Causes: <b>nuclear charge</b> (across) and <b>shielding and distance</b> (down).<br>• Cations are <b>smaller</b>, anions <b>larger</b> than their atoms.<br>• Dips in period 3: Al (3p) and S (paired electrons).	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem05-016	Key points: <b>Families</b>	• Group 1 reactivity <b>increases</b> down. Group 17 reactivity <b>decreases</b> down. A more reactive halogen displaces a less reactive one.<br>• Oxides: Na and Mg basic, Al amphoteric, Si, P and S acidic.<br>• Transition elements: variable oxidation states, coloured compounds, catalysts, complex ions.	ImatPath Chemistry summary
chem05-006	Which order lists these period 3 elements from the smallest atomic radius to the largest?<br><br><br>A) Na, Mg, Al, S, Cl<br>B) Cl, S, Al, Mg, Na<br>C) Cl, S, Al, Na, Mg<br>D) S, Cl, Al, Mg, Na<br>E) Al, Cl, S, Mg, Na	<b>B) Cl, S, Al, Mg, Na</b><br><br>Across a period the nuclear charge increases while the electrons enter the same shell, so the atoms get <b>smaller</b>. The radius is smallest for Cl and largest for Na.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem05-008	The successive ionisation energies of an element in period 3 are 578, 1817, 2745, 11 577 and 14 842 kJ/mol. Which element is it?<br><br><br>A) Aluminium<br>B) Magnesium<br>C) Sodium<br>D) Silicon<br>E) Phosphorus	<b>A) Aluminium</b><br><br>The big jump comes after the 3rd electron (2745 → 11 577), because the 4th electron has to come from an inner shell. So there are 3 valence electrons: group 13, which is aluminium in period 3.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem05-010	A period 3 oxide is insoluble in water, has a very high melting point and reacts with hot concentrated NaOH but not with hydrochloric acid. What is it?<br><br><br>A) Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub><br>B) MgO<br>C) P<sub>4</sub>O<sub>10</sub><br>D) SO<sub>2</sub><br>E) SiO<sub>2</sub>	<b>E) SiO<sub>2</sub></b><br><br>SiO<sub>2</sub> has a giant covalent structure (very high melting point, insoluble) and is <b>acidic</b>: it reacts with bases. Al<sub>2</sub>O<sub>3</sub> is amphoteric (it would also react with the acid). MgO is basic. P<sub>4</sub>O<sub>10</sub> and SO<sub>2</sub> dissolve in water.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
chem05-012	Which of these ions is expected to form coloured compounds in water?<br><br><br>A) Zn<sup>2+</sup><br>B) Sc<sup>3+</sup><br>C) Ca<sup>2+</sup><br>D) Cu<sup>2+</sup><br>E) K<sup>+</sup>	<b>D) Cu<sup>2+</sup></b><br><br>Colour needs a <b>partly filled</b> <i>d</i> subshell. Cu<sup>2+</sup> is 3d<sup>9</sup>. Zn<sup>2+</sup> is 3d<sup>10</sup> (full) and Sc<sup>3+</sup> is [Ar] (empty <i>d</i>), so both are colourless. Ca<sup>2+</sup> and K<sup>+</sup> have no <i>d</i> electrons.	ImatPath Chemistry practice
