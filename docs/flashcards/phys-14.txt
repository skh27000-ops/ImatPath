phys14-001	<b>Conductors and insulators</b>: what is it?	In a <b>conductor</b> (metals, salt water) charges can move freely. In an <b>insulator</b> (plastic, glass, rubber) they cannot.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys14-003	<b>Electric field</b>: what is it?	The <b>electric field</b> at a point is the <b>force per unit positive charge</b> placed there: <i>E = F/q</i>. It is a vector with unit N/C (equal to V/m).	ImatPath Physics definition
phys14-005	<b>Electric potential</b>: what is it?	The <b>electric potential</b> <i>V</i> at a point is the <b>potential energy per unit positive charge</b>, that is the work done in bringing a small positive charge from infinity to that point, divided by the charge. <i>V = E<sub>p</sub>/q</i>. The unit is the <b>volt (V)</b>: 1 V = 1 J/C.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys14-019	<b>conductor</b>: what is it?	In a <b>conductor</b> (metals, salt water) charges can move freely.	ImatPath Physics term
phys14-020	<b>insulator</b>: what is it?	In an <b>insulator</b> (plastic, glass, rubber) they cannot.	ImatPath Physics term
phys14-021	<small>Electric charge</small><br><b>Conservation of charge</b>	the total charge of an isolated system does not change.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys14-022	<small>Electric charge</small><br><b>Quantisation</b>	charge comes in multiples of the <b>elementary charge</b> <i>e = 1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup></i> C. A proton has <i>+e</i>, an electron has <i>-e</i>.	ImatPath Physics key idea
phys14-002	Formula to know: <b>The force between point charges</b>	<i>F = k(q<sub>1</sub>q<sub>2</sub>)/(r<sup>2</sup>) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; k = (1)/(4πε<sub>0</sub>) = 8.99 × 10<sup>9</sup> N m<sup>2</sup>C<sup>-2</sup></i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys14-004	Formula to know: <b>Definition</b>	<i>E = k(Q)/(r<sup>2</sup>)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys14-006	Formula to know: <b>Potential energy and potential</b>	<i>V = k(Q)/(r) &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; E<sub>p</sub> = k(Qq)/(r)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys14-007	Formula to know: <b>Equipotentials and uniform fields</b>	<i>E = (Δ V)/(d)</i>	ImatPath Physics formula
phys14-016	Key points: <b>Charge and Coulomb</b>	• Like repel, unlike attract. <i>e = 1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup></i> C. Charge is conserved and quantised.<br>• <i>F = kq<sub>1</sub>q<sub>2</sub>/r<sup>2</sup></i>, <i>k = 8.99 × 10<sup>9</sup></i> N m<sup>2</sup>C<sup>-2</sup>. Inverse square, vector addition.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys14-017	Key points: <b>Field</b>	• <i>E = F/q</i> (N/C or V/m). Point charge <i>E = kQ/r<sup>2</sup></i>. Force on a charge <i>F = qE</i>.<br>• Lines: start on +, end on −, never cross, closer = stronger. Parallel plates: uniform field.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys14-018	Key points: <b>Potential</b>	• <i>V = kQ/r</i> (scalar, add with signs). <i>E<sub>p</sub> = qV</i>. <i>W = qΔ V</i>. 1 eV = <i>1.60 × 10<sup>-19</sup></i> J.<br>• Uniform field: <i>E = Δ V/d</i>. Field lines are perpendicular to equipotentials.	ImatPath Physics summary
