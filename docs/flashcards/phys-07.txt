phys07-001	<b>Torque</b>: what is it?	The <b>torque</b> of a force about a pivot is <i>τ = rFsinθ</i>, where <i>r</i> is the distance from the pivot to the point where the force acts, and <i>θ</i> is the angle between <i>r</i> and <i>F</i>. The unit is the <b>newton metre (N m)</b>.	ImatPath Physics definition
phys07-006	<b>Conservation of angular momentum</b>: what is it?	If <b>no external torque</b> acts on a system, its <b>total angular momentum is constant</b>.	ImatPath Physics definition

phys07-015	Key points: <b>Torque and equilibrium</b>	• <i>τ = rFsinθ = F ×</i> lever arm. Unit N m.<br>• Equilibrium: <i>∑ F = 0</i> <b>and</b> <i>∑τ = 0</i>. Clockwise = anticlockwise.<br>• A <b>couple</b>: net force zero, net torque <i>Fd</i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys07-016	Key points: <b>Rotation</b>	• <i>I = ∑ mr<sup>2</sup></i>. <i>τ<sub>net</sub> = Iα</i>.<br>• <i>L = Iω</i>. <i>τ = Δ L/Δ t</i>. KE <i>= (1)/(2)Iω<sup>2</sup></i>.<br>• No external torque: <b><i>L</i> is conserved</b>, so smaller <i>I</i> means larger <i>ω</i>.	ImatPath Physics summary
phys07-007	A uniform 5.0 m ladder of mass 20 kg leans against a smooth vertical wall at 60° to the ground. A 70 kg person stands 3.0 m along the ladder from the base. What is the horizontal force of the wall on the ladder? (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 510 N<br>B) 294 N<br>C) 882 N<br>D) 147 N<br>E) 588 N	<b>B) 294 N</b><br><br>Torques about the base: <i>N<sub>w</sub>(5.0sin 60<sup>°</sup>) = 196(2.5cos 60<sup>°</sup>) + 686(3.0cos 60<sup>°</sup>)</i>. So <i>4.33N<sub>w</sub> = 245 + 1029 = 1274</i> and <i>N<sub>w</sub> = 294</i> N.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys07-009	A solid disc of mass 8.0 kg and radius 0.40 m is at rest. A constant torque of 4.8 N m acts on it for 5.0 s. What is its final rotational kinetic energy?<br><br><br>A) 450 J<br>B) 900 J<br>C) 225 J<br>D) 37.5 J<br>E) 112 J	<b>A) 450 J</b><br><br><i>I = (1)/(2)MR<sup>2</sup> = 0.64</i> kg m<sup>2</sup>. <i>α = τ/I = 7.5</i> rad/s<sup>2</sup>, <i>ω = α t = 37.5</i> rad/s. <i>E<sub>k</sub> = (1)/(2)Iω<sup>2</sup> = 450</i> J.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys07-011	A solid sphere rolls without slipping from rest down a slope, dropping through a height of 1.5 m. What is its speed at the bottom? (<i>g = 9.8</i> m/s<sup>2</sup>)<br><br><br>A) 5.42 m/s<br>B) 3.83 m/s<br>C) 4.43 m/s<br>D) 5.5 m/s<br>E) 4.58 m/s	<b>E) 4.58 m/s</b><br><br>Energy: <i>mgh = (1)/(2)mv<sup>2</sup> + (1)/(2)Iω<sup>2</sup></i> with <i>I = (2)/(5)mR<sup>2</sup></i> and <i>ω = v/R</i>. So <i>gh = (7)/(10)v<sup>2</sup></i> and <i>v = √(10gh/7) = 4.58</i> m/s.	ImatPath Physics practice
phys07-013	A force <i>F = (3, 4)</i> N acts at the position <i>r = (2, 1)</i> m relative to a pivot at the origin. What is the torque about the pivot?<br><br><br>A) 11 N m, anticlockwise<br>B) 5 N m, clockwise<br>C) 10 N m, anticlockwise<br>D) 5 N m, anticlockwise<br>E) 11 N m, clockwise	<b>D) 5 N m, anticlockwise</b><br><br><i>τ<sub>z</sub> = xF<sub>y</sub> - yF<sub>x</sub> = (2)(4) - (1)(3) = 8 - 3 = 5</i> N m. The sign is positive, which means anticlockwise.	ImatPath Physics practice
