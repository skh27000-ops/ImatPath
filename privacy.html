Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data ImatPath collects and why. ImatPath is run by Mohamed El Mounir Belkacemi, Algeria, who is responsible for your data. Contact: imatpathpro@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, your name, and your login method (email and password, or Google sign-in). We never see your Google password.
  • Document access: which course documents your account opens or downloads, and when. We use this to protect our content and to handle refund requests.
  • Study data: your progress, answers, flashcard reviews, mock exam results, settings and the days you use the platform, so that you can continue on any device and we can run the service.
  • Purchase data: when you buy, our payment provider collects your payment details and billing information. We receive only your email, the product bought, the order reference and the payment status. We never receive or store your card number.
  • Messages: what you send us by email.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and run the platform.
  • To give you access after you pay, and to handle refunds.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

Who processes your data

We use trusted providers to run ImatPath: Supabase (accounts and database), Cloudflare (website hosting), Google (optional sign-in), and our payment provider (payments and receipts). They process data only to provide their service. Your data may be stored or processed in countries outside your own.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage and similar technology to keep you signed in and remember your settings and progress. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and study data while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as accounting and tax rules require. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to how we use it. Write to imatpathpro@gmail.com from the email address of your account and we will reply within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

ImatPath is meant for students preparing for university entrance. If you are under 16, please use it with a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
