for each correct answer

0

for each wrong answer

Example: 40 correct, 10 wrong, 10 blank

40 × 1.5 − 10 × 0.4 = 56 out of 90

How to choose

The city

You will spend at least six years there. Choose a city you can live in, not a ranking.

The cost

Rent for a one-bedroom in the centre goes from about €475 to about €1,472 a month.

The score

Cut-offs differ by university, for EU and non-EU, and change every year.

Where you actually study

Milano-Bicocca: Bergamo. Parma: Piacenza. Turin: Orbassano. Padova: Venice.

Watch out

Your name must match your passport everywhere.

A declaration of value or a CIMEA statement: ask which one your university accepts. Catania did not need it at pre-enrolment in 2026.

IB or A-levels not final yet? Upload provisional results. The final diploma is needed before enrolment.

Source: Locomotive, MedSchool.it

Pre-enrolment, pre-registration, pre-evaluation

Pre-enrolment

The government step on Universitaly. Mandatory for non-EU students living abroad. You need it for the visa.

Pre-registration

An extra step some universities ask for on their own website. Check if yours does.

Pre-evaluation

The university checks your file first (in 2026: Bari, Messina, Marche, Turin, Bicocca). It is not admission: your IMAT score decides that.

Each university sets its own steps and dates. Read your university’s call before you start.

Sources: Locomotive, MedSchool.it

Pre-evaluation: who asks for it

The university checks your foreign qualification before it validates your Universitaly pre-enrolment. It is not admission.

What goes wrong in pre-enrolment

1

Documents do not meet that university’s criteria. Real people review your file.

2

Wrong personal data. You cannot edit it: send a support ticket on Universitaly.

3

Two universities. Non-EU: one only. Ask the first to cancel, then re-apply.

4

A missed university deadline.

!

Validation can take up to 90 days. Validated is not admitted, and not a visa.

Three mistakes that can get you rejected

1

University mismatch

Pre-enrolment university and IMAT first choice must be the same one.

2

Missed deadline

Each university has its own date. Late can cost you the whole year.

3

Incomplete documents

Name must match the passport. Missing paper: your file can be put on hold.

All three are easy to avoid.

Rules vary by university. Always check your university’s official call.

Regional scholarships

What they give

A tuition waiver, subsidised or free housing, daily meals and a yearly cash grant.

Who decides

A regional right-to-study agency (for example DiSCo in Lazio), not the university. Based on family income (ISEE).

Lazio example

Up to €7,557 a year for students living away from home, if ISEE is below €28,339. International students with family abroad count as living away.

Every region has its own agency, rules and dates. In 2026 the Lazio application closed on 22 July.

Source: Locomotive scholarship guide, Pava Education

Your routeStep by step

If you are EU, this is your route

Who counts

EU citizens, and anyone legally resident in Italy.

Also EU-equated: Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Switzerland, Holy See, San Marino, and non-EU holders of an EU long-term or eligible Italian residence permit.

The steps

1. Register on Universitaly, 26 Aug–9 Sep (15:00).
2. Rank test venue and universities.
3. Pay the fee and sit the IMAT.
No visa pre-enrolment, and you can rank several universities.

The rules

Minimum score to be ranked: 20 points (non-EU abroad: above 0).
EU and non-EU have separate rankings.
Under 27 at some universities: extra courses (OFA).

In 2026/27, 1,207 seats went to EU and Italy residents, 845 to non-EU abroad.

Sources: Locomotive, MUR decree 1005

Your route, one step at a time

STEP 1

Confirm your status

EU citizen, EU-equated, or legally resident in Italy. Equivalent status needs proof uploaded (Bologna: a drop-down plus documents).

STEP 2

Register on Universitaly

26 Aug–9 Sep 2026, 15:00. Rank several universities, pick a test centre, declare any certificate, pay the fee.

STEP 3

Foreign diploma?

Bicocca asks everyone for Apply@unimib by 9 Sep. Pavia checks it with a €35 fee before enrolment.

STEP 4

Sit the IMAT

29 Sep 2026, 13:30 Italian time. Valid ID and payment receipt.

STEP 5

Scores and ranking

Scores 8 Oct. Minimum 20 points to be ranked. National ranking 26 Oct: assigned or reserved.

STEP 6

Enrol and confirm

4 working days to enrol. Confirm interest in 5 days at every update, or you are excluded.

Sources: MUR Decree 1005, Locomotive, university calls

Two routes, different steps

EU and EU-equivalent

Register for the IMAT on Universitaly and rank several universities.

No pre-enrolment and no study visa.

EU-equivalent: for example non-EU citizens with an eligible Italian residence permit.

Non-EU, living abroad

Pre-enrol on Universitaly, for ONE university and one degree.

The university validates your file and sends it to the embassy you chose.

Then you apply for the type D study visa.

Florence (2026): 50 places for EU and equivalent applicants, none for non-EU applicants abroad.

Sources: Locomotive, MedSchool.it, Italy Medical Schools

Before the exam

1

Choose ONE university

Your pre-enrolment and IMAT first choice must match. You compete only where you pre-enrolled.

2

Pre-evaluation, if asked

Bari, Messina, Marche, Turin, Bicocca, and Pavia if you test there. Upload your documents on its portal. 2026: 30 Jun to 11 Sep.

3

Pre-enrol on Universitaly

Free and official. The university validates up to its non-EU quota plus 20%, then validations pause.

4

Register for the IMAT

On Universitaly, 26 Aug–9 Sep 2026, 15:00 Italian time. Pick a test centre, declare any language certificate, pay the fee. Irrevocable at the deadline.

Sources: MUR circular 2026/27, Decree 1005, Locomotive

Exam, ranking, visa, arrival

5

Sit the IMAT

29 Sep 2026, 13:30 Italian time, on paper. Bring valid ID and your payment receipt.

6

Results and ranking

Scores 8 Oct, ranking 26 Oct. If assigned, enrol within 4 working days. Confirm you stay on the list within 5 days, or you are out.

7

Study visa (type D)

Apply after validation. MUR deadline: 30 Nov 2026 (Bicocca: 28 Feb 2027). Needs funds, housing and insurance.

8

Arrive and settle

Residence permit request within 8 working days. Foreign-diploma documents by your university’s date.

Sources: MUR circular 2026/27, Decree 1005, Locomotive

What the study visa asks for

Money

At least €10,179.85 a year, from legal, traceable sources. A scholarship counts only with an award letter.

Housing

Confirmed accommodation. Only applying for a residence hall does not count.

Health insurance

Cover for care and hospital stays in Italy, with no limits on urgent admission.

Your file

University validation, passport, and a provisional diploma certificate if you have no diploma yet. Self-declarations are not accepted.

Type D visa, valid 365 days. Deadline for 2026/27: 30 Nov 2026. Residence permit within 8 working days of arrival.

Source: MUR circular 2026/27 and 2027/28

Documents and deadlines

From Algeria: “I worry the most about the documents and the deadlines.”

Two lists, two habits

The checklist: passport, photo, diplomas and transcripts, translated. The deadline: your university’s own date. Put both in your calendar.

From Tunisia: “What documents, and where? Which universities recognise the IB diploma?”

Universitaly, and each university

You upload on Universitaly. The IB is accepted as a valid diploma, with a declaration of value or CIMEA statement. No final result yet? Upload provisional results. Each university sets its details: read its call.

Pre-enrolment questions

From Bangladesh: “Required high school GPA or percentage?”

No national minimum: non-EU applicants are ranked on the IMAT score, not school grades. You need a 12-year diploma. Some universities may set minimum grades: read the call.

“Pre-evaluation or pre-enrolment? Which universities need which?”

Pre-enrolment is the Universitaly step for non-EU. Some add pre-evaluation. In 2026: Bari, Messina, Marche, Turin, Bicocca. See Deadlines, and check each call.

“Why can a pre-enrolment be rejected?”

Documents that miss the criteria, wrong data, two universities, a missed deadline. See Documents.

Choosing and the score

From Bangladesh: “How can I select which university? How does scoring and ranking work?”

City, cost, cut-offs

Pick the city you can live in, check the rent, then look at last year’s cut-offs. One national ranking: your score decides your seat.

From Tunisia: “The score.”

+1.5, 0, −0.4

60 questions, 100 minutes, 90 points maximum. A wrong answer costs 0.4, a blank costs nothing.
